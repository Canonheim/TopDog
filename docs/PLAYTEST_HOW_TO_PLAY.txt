TOP DOG - playtest build 0.3.1
==============================

Climb the barrel, then the tower of everyone who went before you. Grab, fling,
freeze. Lock in above the line before the clock runs out.

STARTING
- Double-click TopDog.exe. Keep the two DLL files next to it.
- Windows may warn about an unknown app (it isn't signed yet): More info > Run anyway.
- "TopDog (no Steam).bat" starts the game with Steam switched off. Use it if a
  controller doesn't respond: on Valve's test app Steam can take the controller.

CONTROLS
Hold a limb's button to grab that hand or foot and drag it toward the stick or
the mouse. Let go and it sticks to whatever it's touching. You can only move a
limb while another one is stuck (or a friend is holding you).

                 Controller      Keyboard + mouse
  Left hand      X               A
  Right hand     A               D
  Left foot      Y               W
  Right foot     B               S
  Steer          left stick      mouse
  Lock in        Start           Space
  Pause          Back            Esc

The buttons are drawn on the hands and feet. Bright = held, green = stuck.
Keyboard, mouse and controller swap any time in Practice.
F1 opens the feel panel (every physics number, live) if you want to tinker.

MODES
- Practice: climb alone, no clock.
- Play together (this PC): each controller or the keyboard joins as a player.
- Tower: take turns; lock in above the top before your clock runs out, or
  you're out (your body stays in the tower).
- Scramble: everyone at once; every lock-in raises the line.

PLAYING ONLINE TOGETHER
Both of you need Steam running and logged in.
1. One of you: Play online > Host. Then Invite friends (or the friend can find
   the game in the lobby list with Refresh).
2. The other: Play online > Refresh, and Join the game.
3. Everyone presses Ready; the host presses Start.
If Steam won't connect, the LAN option works on the same network (Host on LAN,
then the other types the host's IP and presses Join on LAN).
Controller online: works through Steam now. The bottom line of the menus says
"Steam Input: 1 controller" when Steam has your pad.

Found a bug? Note what you did right before it, and a screenshot helps.
