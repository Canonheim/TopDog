# What each area's author reported (their doubts, in their words, shortened)

Nobody has played any of this. These are the things each author asked to have tested first.

## A3 La Gruta de Cristal
- Builds clean, leak 0. The route folds once (east along the floor, back west up the stair, east again over the start hall on the chandeliers).
- Changes: 3.6's catch is a crystal raft (a `sink` mover) that carries a faller back under 3.4's ledge; 3.9's miss costs one ledge, not two; 3.8 rises 32 degrees (its top must be a legal slide); the whole horn of 3.10 is grip.
- Doubts: 3.1's launches are top corners (the body can't go right round); 3.8's patches are 1.0 m (the guide for D4 is 1.5); 3.2's miss may catch a nub and cost less than designed; 3.9 fallers leave the plate at 6 to 10 m/s and might bounce into the pendulum pit.
- Test first: riding the geode's lining over the top and out; whether the raft starts under a limp body; the 3.9 chute; ring to ring; the 11 m needle fling; the pendulum's 9 m/s bottom speed.
- Sound: `SFX.md` lists the weak sounds (vulture, creaks, thunder, water first).

## A1 La Mina Vieja
- Builds clean, leak 0. East mass (gallery and ladder shaft), a catwalk and rope over the water, a pillar on an arch, open water to the fall, the upper works beside the fall's top 30 m.
- Changes: 1.2's third gap is 2.05 m (2.3 broke the F1 limit); 1.5's lift runs on a clock; 1.8's beams are 2.5 m apart end to end with height steps making them F2; 1.9's wheel is 6 m (the kit's size) and its miss is a slip; 1.10 is mostly a slip; the Sump is x -248 to -188.
- Not met: the wall lunges of 1.2, 1.6, 1.10 have no clear launch circle (they are lunges); most rest ledges only have drips for their unease.
- Test first: 1.7 (does a raised hand take a chain as the cart goes off the trestle: the least certain piece); 1.11's 3.4 s dry windows; the wheel's rim from the staging (a 1.86 m reach); the rocks' timing in 1.10; stepping off the counterweight in its 2.5 s at the top; the first reach to the rope (1.84 m); the 50 m swim back across the Sump.

## The music (docs/peak/MUSIC.md)
- Twelve loops, 98 bpm (not the 96 first planned), 32 bars, exactly 3,456,000 samples each; one chord plan, one tune, the motif D F# A B resolved only at the summit; all at the match music's loudness. Three stingers.
- Nobody has heard any of it. Listen first for: the mode crossings between neighbours (a2 with a3, a8 with a9, a0 with a1, a4 with a5, a6 with a7); whether a10 is tense or just ugly; a8's deliberate lateness; a0's low-pass; one tune for the whole climb wearing thin.
- Stand-ins from the soundfont: anvils (agogo and ride bell), pick-axes (side stick, triangle, wood block), clay flute (ocarina), glass harmonica (a pad).
- The temple's phantom blocks (5.9) run on the course's own clock: they are NOT in time with the music.
