Rest 2.0: The frog's valve chamber

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

A dry valve chamber, cut in the brick under the fitters' gallery. The keeper of the pool worked the outlet valve from here. It is a round brick vault 3 m wide and 2.6 m tall, and you walk into it from the east end of the ledge where El Charco del Pozo starts. Its round iron door was left ajar. You walk the gallery above without knowing it is there. When you drop to the ledge and turn round, the door is behind you. The frog lives in it.

What must not change

  • Nothing on the route changes. The ledge, the drop on to it and the start of El Charco del Pozo stay as they are.
  • The room is new floor: 3 m of it, level with the ledge, east of the ledge's end. Today that is solid.
  • The frog is friendly only. Nothing to press, nothing gained (note 60).

The three layers

Background

  • 1The pool hall's east wall and the gallery's wall. The brick these two places already have behind them, drawn so you can see where the room sits: under the gallery, beside the ledge. (2.8 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 2The room. Cut into brick that is solid today. Floor, back wall and a round barrel vault. No front wall: you see into it. Dry pale brick, the only dry room in the biome. (1.2 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 3The round iron door. A riveted round door 1.9 m across with the pug cast in its middle and a hand wheel. It is hinged on the back jamb and stands half open against the pool hall's wall, out of your way. Its cast ring frame is set in the wall's end. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 4The outlet valve. Against the room's back wall, behind where you walk: a fat pipe up through the floor and into the east wall, with a big valve wheel on it at chest height and a round gauge above. (1.6 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 5The frog. Sitting on the keeper's upturned bucket, 0.3 m tall, in a tiny oilskin hat. It breathes, blinks, watches you and croaks. You can hear it from the gallery above. (1.5 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 6The keeper's lantern. A works lantern on a hook in the vault, lit. A patch of greenish light falls out of the door on to the ledge: that is how you find the room. (2.1 to 2.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7The door's sill. The ledge's stone coping runs on through the door's ring as a worn sill, level, with no step. The room's floor is dry pale brick.
