Challenge 2.5: Las Compuertas

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The gate hall. Three sluice gates stand in the back wall, one above the next like a flight of locks, and each let water on to one floor of the mill. A gate is too heavy to wind up by hand, so the company stood a water-pressure ram in front of each one. The works' regulator still runs them: pressure from the lake lifts a ram slowly, holds it, then the valve dumps and the gate's own weight slams it shut. What you ride is the head of a ram.

What must not change

  • Three heads, each 2 m wide, on the three steps, rising 3.2 m and slamming, on today's timing.
  • Only the riveted top of a head can be held. Everything under it is slick.
  • The landing above them at 121 m.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The gate hall's back wall. Brick, 20 m tall, wet and black low down where the gates leak, paler above. (2.4 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 2The three gates. Behind each ram: a sluice gate in a cast-iron frame, with the pug cast in the frame's head and a rack and gear, seized, beside it. The gate's leaf is yoked to the ram's head and goes up and down with it. While a gate is up you see black water moving behind it. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 3The regulator and its pipes. A riveted valve chest high on the wall, and a fat green pipe from it down to each ram. This is what drives them, and you can see it. (1.6 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 4A works lantern. On a wall bracket beside the regulator. Steady. (2.1 to 2.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 5Housing, ram and head. Note 19: each rising block is redrawn as a machine piston, the same size and with the same top. A fixed iron housing stands on the step where the block rests today. A broad polished ram, as wide as the head, comes up out of it carrying the head. The ram is slick and can't be held.
  • 6The heads. The part you hold: a thick riveted plate in rust orange with a raised rim, drawn on today's top.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 7The regulator's supply pipe. One green riser in front of the solid rock east of the hall, from the frame's bottom to its top. It never crosses the hall. (1.3 to 1.6 m in front)

Small effects, no models
