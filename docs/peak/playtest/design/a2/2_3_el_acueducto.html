Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Challenge 2.3: El Acueducto

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The aqueduct was the wheel's feed: a brick trough on round arches, sloping gently down from the east wall to the top of the wheel. It was built two arches thick. The back half still stands whole. The front half, the half with the trough on it, has lost three spans into the race, and what is left of it is four pier tops with a stump of trough on each. On the wall above every span is a pair of iron rings: the masons' lifting rings, for the tackle that took the trough's plates out for cleaning.

What must not change

  • The four pier tops and the three gaps of 3, 4 and 5 m, each a little higher than the last.
  • The six rings, each exactly where it is. They do not move.
  • The race under the gaps and the hatch ladder up the east wall.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The standing arcade. The aqueduct's back half: the same four piers, with the three round arches still whole between them, and plain brick above the arches up to the roof. It shows what each gap was. The piers under your pier tops are its piers. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 2Black water going back. Through each arch: the race going back under a second, further arcade, lit only by the shimmer off the water. (2.4 to 8.0 m behind you)
  • 3The fallen spans. Heaps of brick under the water at the foot of each gap, behind where you swim. You can't stand on them. (1.3 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 4The hatch. The ladder up the east wall goes through the roof by a brick-lined hatch with a cast iron rim. (1.2 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 5Ring pads and plates. Note 18: the six chains are gone. Behind each ring, a short brick pad built out from the arcade's wall with a riveted iron plate on its face, the way a mooring ring sits on a quay. The pad stops at the back edge of the play space. It is scenery because of where it stands. (1.2 to 1.9 m behind you)
  • 6Two works lanterns. On brackets on the arcade's piers, not hung in the air between the rings. Steady. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7The rings' eye bolts. Each ring stands on a short eye bolt that points straight at you from its plate. From the side the bolt is hidden behind its own ring, so nothing new shows in the play space and nothing new can be held. Nothing hangs above a ring.
  • 8Broken ends. Each pier top shows the trough's section on its broken end, and under it the first few bricks of the arch that sprang from it, drawn on.
  • 9Copings. The pale stone rim of the trough along the top of each pier top. It is what you land on.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
