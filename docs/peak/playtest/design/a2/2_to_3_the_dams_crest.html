Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Join 2 to 3: The dam's crest

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The top of the works, and the end of everything anyone built until the temple. The crest is the dam's walkway, with the lake on its far side under a brick vault. An iron ladder goes up from the crest through a brick shaft in the roof. But the roof is giving way to what is above it: crystals have grown down through the top courses of the brick and split them. The lake is not black. It is clear, and it is lit from below by crystals on its bed, because this water comes from the cave. Teal lantern light reaches as far as the foot of the ladder. Violet light comes down the shaft to meet it. Two steps above the shaft's rim, the brick is gone.

What must not change

  • The crest, the lake and the rungs, exactly as they are.
  • The way out: up the rungs, through the roof, on to the cave's first floor.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The lake's brick vault. Slate-grey brick over the lake, in round-arched bays. Its top three courses are cracked, and pale light shows in the cracks. (4.0 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 2The shaft's lining. Brick, in a round shaft cut in half, from the hall's roof up to the cave's floor. Sound at the bottom, split open by crystal at the top. (1.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 3Crystals grown down through the brick. Rough points of the cave's crystal, deep blue-violet because they are scenery, coming down out of the roof and through the shaft's lining, each with split and shouldered-aside brick round its root. One of them, over the crest, is bright white and is the first of the cave's lights. (1.3 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 4The lake's bed. Clear water, and through it small crystal points on the bed at the back, glowing faintly. They light the lake from below. They are behind where anyone would swim. (2.0 to 5.0 m behind you)
  • 5Violet light down the shaft. A soft violet light falls down the shaft and lies on the lake under it. Where it meets the last lantern's teal, on the crest, the brick is lit in both colours. (1.3 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 6The last lantern and the last pug. On the crest, far back: the dam's sluice wheel on its spindle, with the pug cast in its hub and a crust of crystal on its rim. Over it, the last works lantern. After this there is no iron, no brick and no lantern until the temple. (1.4 to 4.0 m behind you)
  • 7The head of the lift shaft. At the crest's east end: the top of the dam's lift shaft, with its two guide rails and its buffer beam. It belongs to La Presa's page and is drawn here only so the two agree. (1.2 to 1.5 m behind you)
  • 8The cave's first crystals. Above the roof: the clusters that stand on the cave's first floor today. They belong to the crystal cave's own pages and are drawn here only so the two agree. (1.4 to 4.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 9Crusted rungs. The ladder is rust-orange iron from the crest up to the cave's floor. Its last 2 m, above the floor, are crusted with rough pale lilac crystal. It is the same ladder and the same hold. The works' way of saying you can hold it hands over to the cave's way on one rung.
  • 10Split copings. The crest's stone coping is cracked in two places, with a thin seam of crystal in each crack, drawn on. Nothing sticks up: the walk is as flat as today.
  • 11The shaft's rim. Where the ladder comes out on the cave's floor: half a ring of the works' pale stone, and the other half already crystal. Today a small pale block stands here.

Small effects, no models

  • Clear water: you can see the bed, and the lake has no black in it.
  • A slow glitter drifting down the shaft, the first of the cave's.
  • No drips and no steam here. The works' noise stops at the crest.
