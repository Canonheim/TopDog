Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Challenge 2.11: La Presa

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The dam: a wall of brick 15 m tall with the works' lake behind it. Its downstream face leans back, and the keeper went up it on an inclined lift: a small iron cage on a toothed rail laid up the face, with a hand winch in the cage. You stand in it and wind, and the winch's pinion climbs the rail one tooth at a time. Stop winding and it creeps back down. Behind the lift the dam's stepped buttress goes up beside you, with the dam's leak running down its steps.

What must not change

  • Note 25: one lift, from the apron to the crest, in place of the five platforms and five cranks. It rises only while you wind and sinks when you stop.
  • The apron you start from and the crest you step off on to, at the heights they have today.
  • The lake behind the crest and the rungs out through the roof.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The dam hall's back wall. Brick, far back, blue-black. The hall is wide: the dam runs a long way back into the scene. (6.0 to 7.0 m behind you)
  • 2The stepped buttress. Behind the lift: a brick buttress against the dam's face, rising in five steps of 2.9 m, each with a pale stone coping. These are the five steps the place has today, moved back out of the play space. The lift passes in front of them. (1.4 to 5.0 m behind you)
  • 3The leak. Water from a crack near the crest runs down the buttress step by step, a thin white fall on each riser and a film on each tread, and off its foot into the upper reach. (1.4 to 5.0 m behind you)
  • 4The far rail. The lift runs on two rails. The far one lies on the face just behind the play space, toothed, with the pug cast on its foot. (1.2 to 1.4 m behind you)
  • 5The head of the incline. On the crest: a buffer post, and the pawl wheel that clicks as the lift comes up. It says the rail ends here. (1.3 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 6Two works lanterns. On wall brackets, one half way up and one over the crest. They are the last of the works' teal lights. (5.7 to 6.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7One leaning face. Note 25: the five steps in the play space are smoothed into one leaning face of brick, from the apron at 152 m to the crest at 167 m, with a stone coping along its edge. It is slick: the lift is the only way up.
  • 8The lift. Note 25: a level deck 2.4 m wide with an open iron cage, and the winch on a post at its east end, so you face the dam as you wind. Drawn at the apron, and dashed at the crest where you step off. The deck keeps level all the way up.
  • 9The cast pug. On the dam's cut face, where it is today: the First Top Dog, 3 m across, cast in iron and let into the brick. The brick round it gets drawn courses and a slanting crack with white water in it.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 10The near rail. The lift's other rail, lying flat on the leaning face in front of the dam's brick. It is the foreground pole to the far rail's background pole. It lies on solid wall and crosses no open space. (1.2 to 1.4 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • The leak's five little falls, and a film of water on the buttress.
