Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Challenge 2.4: Brazo de Molino

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The wash house, one floor above the wheel. Ore was washed here in a long brick channel, the mill arm, with a steady stream sent down it from a culvert at the west end. Along the far bank stand the round tanks the washed ore settled in. A filler pipe comes out of the wall over the landing, runs out over the channel's drain and ends in an open spout, still dribbling. The washers got out of the channel by iron rungs in the west wall: the top two are left.

What must not change

  • The channel: 14 m, its steady current east, and the drain at its east end that drops you to the race.
  • The filler pipe you go out along, hand over hand, before you drop in.
  • The two rungs at the west end and the two landings.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The wash house's back wall. Brick, with two round arches barred with iron. The far background, blue-black, shows through the bars. (4.4 to 5.0 m behind you)
  • 2The inlet culvert. A round arch at the waterline at the west end, with a rack of iron bars across it. The channel's water slides out of it. It is where the current comes from. (1.2 to 4.4 m behind you)
  • 3The far bank and the settling tanks. A stone-coped bank just above the water, and on it two round iron tanks, 2.5 m across, riveted, dull blue-grey. A green pipe goes from each tank up into the roof. (1.6 to 4.2 m behind you)
  • 4The filler pipe's wall end. Over the landing the pipe turns back into the wall through a teal valve wheel. Two wall brackets carry it out over the drain. (1.2 to 4.4 m behind you)
  • 5A works lantern. On a wall bracket at the west end, over the rungs you are swimming for. Steady. (4.1 to 4.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 6The spout. The pipe's west end is drawn as a flared open mouth turned down, with a thin dribble of water falling from it. It marks the place to let go.
  • 7Drowned rungs. Under the two rungs you can hold, three more show below the waterline, green and bent. The two are the top of a ladder.
  • 8Copings. Pale stone copings on both landings.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 9The channel's near wall, cut down. One broken course of brick along the bottom, below the channel's bed and in front of solid rock. It says the channel has a near side. It is lower than the water's bed, so the whole swim is in clear view. (1.3 to 1.8 m in front)
