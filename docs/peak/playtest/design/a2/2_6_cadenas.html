Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Challenge 2.6: Cadenas

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The catch chamber of El Desague, the works' great drain. A riveted iron pipe 5 m across comes down through the roof, and under it an iron hopper funnels whatever falls into a wider pipe that drops to the river. The company opened the chamber for repairs: they lifted plates out of the hopper with three chain hoists on a crane beam, and stacked the plates in the gallery. Then the works flooded and nobody came back. The three chains still hang, over the hole.

What must not change

  • The three chains, where they hang and how long they are.
  • The slick hopper under them and the pipe below it: a miss goes down to the fast river.
  • The landing you leave from and the gallery you land on.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The chamber's back wall. Brick, with one round arch barred with iron high on the west side. The wall carries on down behind the hopper and the lower pipe. (3.6 to 4.2 m behind you)
  • 2The hopper's back half. Riveted iron plates, dull green-black and wet, sloping down to the lower pipe. Two plates are missing and brick shows through. The play space cuts the hopper in half: this is the half behind you. (1.2 to 3.6 m behind you)
  • 3The upper pipe's mouth. A heavy bolted flange round the hole in the roof, with the company's pug cast in it. The back half of the ring shows. (1.2 to 3.6 m behind you)
  • 4The crane beam and its three hoists. An iron beam under the roof, from wall to wall, with a hoist block on it over each chain. Each chain you hold comes down from its block. No other chain or loop hangs from the beam. (1.3 to 1.7 m behind you)
  • 5The lifted plates. Three curved hopper plates leaning against the gallery's back wall, where the fitters left them. (1.4 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 6A works lantern. On a wall bracket over the landing. Today a lantern hangs in the air between the chains: it moves to the wall, so the only things hanging in this room are the three chains. (3.3 to 3.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7The hopper's cut edge. The slope you can see is the hopper cut through: a line of rivets and a plate seam along it, black and greased, drawn on.
  • 8Shackles. Each chain ends at the top in a big shackle on its hoist's hook, drawn at the roof. It hangs from a thing, not from the rock.

Nothing.
