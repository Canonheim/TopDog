Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Challenge 2.10: La Cascada Chica

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The weir hall. The upper reach ends here and goes over a stone-coped weir, 15 m down into a pool. The keepers had a way up beside the fall: a flight of stone treads built into the back wall, then an iron ladder up the wall, then a second flight to the catwalk. Each tread is a long stone with one end in the wall, and it is the free ends you hold. The ladder between the two flights has rusted away to stumps. Someone who came to climb the Peak hung a rope there instead, from the lifting beam under the roof. The fall's spray wets the treads in turn.

What must not change

  • The seven holds, where they are, and the order the spray wets them in.
  • The one rope: where it hangs, and that its end is where it is.
  • The pool under the climb and the ledge you start from.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The weir hall's back wall. Brick, 23 m tall, black and streaming at the west end beside the fall, drier to the east. A round culvert mouth at the pool and a round barred eye high up. (2.6 to 3.6 m behind you)
  • 2The keepers' stair. On the back wall behind your holds: the stone string of the lower flight, with each tread running back into it, then the two rails of the dead iron ladder with a stump where every rung was, then the string of the upper flight. You can see the whole way up the keepers had. Only its tread ends are left to you. (1.2 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 3The weir, carried on behind. The weir's stone lip and the white sheet of the fall run back into the scene for the hall's whole depth. The fall you climb beside is its near edge. (1.2 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 4The lifting beam. An iron beam under the roof with a ring on it, once used to lift the weir's boards. The rope is tied to the ring. It is a climber's rope, pale and dry, not the works' iron. (1.2 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 5Two works lanterns. On wall brackets: one over the ledge you start from, one high on the east side. Steady, and haloed by the mist. (2.3 to 2.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 6Tread ends. Each hold is drawn as the squared end of a stone tread, 0.55 m wide, pale, with a worn nosing. While the spray is on it, it goes dark and shines.
  • 7The ledge's coping. A pale stone coping on the ledge over the pool.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
