Las Aguas Negras · Area and background design

Challenge 2.2: La Rueda

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The wheel pit. The race runs through the bottom of the mill and turns a water wheel, and the wheel turned everything else: it is one of a pair on the same axle, and the axle runs back through the wall to the mill's gearing. The mill is flooded and nothing is geared to it any more, but the race still runs, so the wheel still turns. You come up the wheelwright's ladder well, step on to his landing, and ride a paddle up.

What must not change

  • The wheel: 4 m across, six spokes, where it is and turning as it turns today.
  • The landing beside it and the ladder up the well.
  • The race under it: fall off and it carries you east to the drain and back to the lip ledge.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The pit's back wall and the wheel arch. Brick to the roof. Behind the wheel a round arch 5.4 m wide opens in it, with the race coming out of the dark under it. This is where the race's water comes from. (2.4 to 5.0 m behind you)
  • 2The second wheel. The same wheel again, 4 m further back on the same axle, turning with yours in the dark of the arch. It is greyer and wetter than the one you ride. On the sketch it is the ring round your wheel's spokes. (3.8 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 3The wheel's frame. A cast-iron frame standing in the race just behind the wheel, with the axle's bearing at its top. Nothing stands in front of the wheel. (1.4 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 4The ladder well's lining. A round brick well cut in half, 1.8 m across, with the iron rungs you climb let into it and a cast ring where it opens at the landing. (1.2 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 5A works lantern. On a wall bracket over the arch. Steady: nothing in this biome's light pulses (note 17). The moving light here is the slow shimmer the race throws up on the underside of the arch. (2.2 to 2.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 6Paddle boards and the hub. Each spoke ends in a paddle board, drawn on its end, and the hub gets an iron boss with the company's pug cast in it. The boards lift water and drop it as they come round.
  • 7The landing's coping. A pale stone coping on the brick landing, like every place you stand in the works.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
