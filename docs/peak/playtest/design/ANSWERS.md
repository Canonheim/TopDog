# Parker's answers to the design questions (11-13)

His exact words, as he gave them, question by question. Not yet applied to the designs
unless a line says so.

1. **Station: a board of crossed-out masks on the station wall?**
   "Feel free to completely rework the station. Add a few Missing posters in the style of the money we for the lock in effects, they are pixel art using geometry"

2. **Station: the frog in plain sight?** (and 3, **should the brake lever work?**, answered with it)
   "In plain sight, and also make it so you have to pull the lever to start the cart, regardless of if you are in the cart. If you pull the lever it goes. It controls like the chair whaere you are hlding the activated limb"
   - This is a change to the play, so it also becomes a playtest note for the to-do list.

4. **The ride: does the cart really jump a broken rail?**
   "real hop and make dust come off the wheels when it lands"
   - A change to the play: also a playtest note.

5. **Tunnel to mine: the 0.6 m step at the door?** Picked: "Keep the step, with a sill".

6. **Mine: background timber greyer and cooler, honey wood always a hold?** Picked: "Yes, that rule".

7. **Mine: the waterfall in the back of nearly every area?** Picked: "Only where it really is".

8. **The Sump's frog cave: how wide?**
   "whatever works, you have complete authority to arrange and even move geometry in play space a bit."
   - A standing permission. Because of it I did not ask the questions that are only about
     arranging things, and decided them myself: 14, 19 and 29 (where each frog's room goes:
     as designed), 23 (the hut is at the 318 m floor, which the picture matches), 25 (the
     temple's beam is made longer so it reaches both chains), 35 (the great iron mask is
     remade taller so its nose and mouth line up).

9. **Mine, El Cubo: the lift shown as worked by water?** Picked: "No, keep it plain".

10. **Mine, La Cortina: holds as timber peg ends, or rock knobs?** "Something else"
    Then, from three ideas, picked: "Iron staples" (rust-orange U-shaped iron rungs hammered into the wet rock by the miners, some bent).

11. **Black waters: green pipe is scenery, orange pipe is a hold?**
    "If it is in the play space you should generally be able to hold it. That is more telling than the color,"
    - A rule for the whole mountain, not just pipes: where a thing stands tells you more
      than its colour. Scenery stays out of the play space; what is in it can be held
      (unless it is plainly dark and wet).

12. **Round arches only in the black waters, stepped arches only in the temple?** Picked: "No such rule".
    - 13 (keep the name Las Compuertas) not asked: the name stays.

15. **Foot of the well: one fallen thing from each place above?** Picked: "Yes, the three fallen things".

16. **The dam's one lift (note 25): up the leaning face, or straight up a shaft?** Picked: "Straight up a shaft".
    - This changes note 25's plan, which had it on the leaning face.

17. **Crystal cave: its moving things as temple work, or purely natural?** Picked: "Purely natural".
    - The cave's sheet and its areas were drawn with temple work; they have to be redone without it.

18. **Crystal cave: pale rough lilac you can hold, indigo glass you can't?** Picked: "Yes, that split".

20. **Crystal cave, El Prisma: a faint fan of rainbow colours on the wall behind the shelves?** Picked: "Keep the rainbow".

21. **Crystal cave, La Columna: how should the three turning rings look?** Picked: "Collars round the column"
    (rings that wrap right round the column like bracelets; I told him they would be seen edge-on, so the play would need rethinking).
    - A change to the play of 3.9: it needs a plan of its own, and becomes a playtest note.

22. **Mushroom garden: one kind of mushroom, or a second kind for scenery?** Picked: "A second kind for scenery".

24. **The frog's hut: a solid roof you can land on?** Picked: "Solid roof".

26. **Temple: a jade band round everything that moves?** Picked: "Yes, a jade band".

27. **Temple: a frieze of small carved figures doing each challenge?**
    "I like the idea but naw we won't do that"
    - The frieze stays as pugs, belts and bones, with no figures doing the challenge.
    - 30 (paw prints over each ghost block giving its count) not asked: it is the same kind of hint, so it goes too.

28. **Temple, the hall of statues: the east forearm that looks holdable and isn't?**
    "Right now the arm on the right is background, so you fall through it. I like that design, make it so that part of the arm has broken and only the hand is sticking from the background and make the second statue have both arms broken so it only has hands, making it harder"
    - A change to the play of 5.5 (the second statue's holds become two hands only): also a playtest note.

31. **Chimney: the temple smiths' forge where the First Top Dog's belt was made?** Picked: "Yes, the belt's forge".

32. **Chimney: light from furnace red at the bottom to daylight at the top, no torches above the forge floor?** Picked: "Agreed".

33. **The smith's hut: a real dog as well as the frozen climber in his dog mask?**
    "only the frog"

34. **The smith's hut: stone and iron with no timber, or a timber and iron lean-to?** Picked: "Stone and iron, no timber".

36. **Chimney, El Fuelle: an open box of dynamite behind the rungs, to explain the dynamite rung?** Picked: "Yes, the box".
