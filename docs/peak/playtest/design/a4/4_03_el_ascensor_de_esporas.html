El Jardín de Hongos · Area and background design

Challenge 4.3: El Ascensor de Esporas

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Half way up the slime ramp there is a hollow, and in the hollow grows a bed of spore pods, each as fat as a barrel. They breathe out in turn, one after another, and their breath is a column of warm air full of pale gold spores, going straight up for 15 m before it thins out. What is left drifts on up to the underside of the island, 9 m higher, where the spores have settled as gold fur. Three vines hang down out of that fur with dust on their ends, into the top of the column. So the lift has a bottom you can see making it, sides you can see, and a top you can see it reach for.

What must not change

  • The column: 4.6 m wide, from 277 m to 292.5 m. Strong in its lower half, weak in its upper half.
  • The three vines whose ends hang at 291.5 m, and the shelf at 295 m beside them.
  • The ramp under it all: drift out of the column and you slide back to Sombreros.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The dust shadow on the back wall. Years of spores have stained the back wall pale gold in a stripe exactly as wide as the column, spreading out where it meets the roof. Even between puffs, you can see where the column is and how high it goes. (6.0 to 6.5 m behind you)
  • 2The forest, far back. One stand of three bell mushrooms on the back of the ramp, thin and pale blue-green, well to the west of the column. (7.0 to 10.0 m behind you)
  • 3The pod bed. Five spore pods in a row on the ramp, just behind where you step in, under the whole width of the column. Each swells, then puffs a ball of spores upward, one after another, so the bed never stops. This is what makes the lift (note 38). (1.3 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 4The furred roof and the vines' root. Above the column the island's underside is furred pale gold with settled spores. In the middle of the fur is a knot of root as big as a cart wheel, and the three vines hang from it. (1.2 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 5The two shelves' stalks. The shelf you step off stands on a short stalk rooted in the ramp. The shelf you climb onto stands on a tall thin one, 15 m, also from the ramp, well clear of the column. (1.2 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 6A lantern pod. On the tall stalk under the top shelf. With the glow of the pod bed below, the column is lit from both ends. (1.3 to 2.2 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7The column, made visible. A thick stream of pale gold spores with soft edges, so you can see where to step in and where you would drop out. The spores rise fast and thick below 285 m and slow and thin above it, so you can see where the lift gives out. Small leaves and motes are carried up in it, and your climber's arms and legs drift upward while you are inside (note 38).
  • 8Dusted vine ends. The last metre of each of the three vines is furred gold, the same as the roof. They are the first thing the spores reach.
  • 9A dusted lip. The east lip of the shelf you step off is gold with spores for its last half metre. It marks the step in.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • The column of spores itself: dense and fast below, thin and slow above, with soft edges.
  • Each pod swells for a moment before it puffs.
  • A soft rushing sound that fades in as you enter the column and out as you leave it.
  • Leaves caught in the column, going round and up.
