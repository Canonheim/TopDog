El Jardín de Hongos · Area and background design

Rest 4.0: The frog's hut

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

East of the snail wall, across the gap, there is a quiet floor of moss at 318 m where nothing happens. It is where you land if you fly wide off the giant mushroom, and a root arm on its lip is the way back. It is also where the frog lives. The frog has hollowed out the stump of a fat old stalk and kept its cap on for a roof, and the moss has grown right over the cap. The side that faces you is open, like a doll's house: you walk in along the floor, and you are standing in its one room. The roof is solid: you can land on it, stand on it, and pull yourself up onto it by an eave. Behind the hut is a row of bell sprouts in a straight line, the only tidy thing in the garden. A snail sleeps beside it.

What must not change

  • The floor, the lip, and the root arm with its 4.2 m reach over the gap: the way back to the shelf under the snail wall.
  • Nothing here can hurt you or cost you height. It is a place to stop.
  • The hut's walls and everything in it stay behind you. Only its roof is in the play space.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The forest. Three stands of bell mushrooms, thin and pale blue-green, 6 to 10 m back, with mossy boulders at their feet. The tallest stand is behind the hut, so the hut sits under a little wood. The big orange scenery mushroom and the two smaller ones that stand on this floor today are gone: nothing orange grows in the scenery (Parker, 11-13). (6.0 to 10.0 m behind you)
  • 2Shelf fungus up the east wall. Three brackets growing from the room's east wall in steps, 11 m up. Today one hangs there alone. (1.4 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 3The frog's snail. Asleep beside the hut, 4 m back, shell 1.8 m across. It is the one that stands here today. (3.4 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 4The frog's garden row. Six bell sprouts in a straight row behind the hut, knee high and pale blue-green, on a strip of turned moss. Somebody planted them. (2.6 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 5The hut's back wall. The back half of a hollow stalk, 3 m wide and 2.6 m tall inside, cream, with a round window in it. A round door of bark stands open against it at the west end. This is the only made thing in the garden. (1.2 to 2.7 m behind you)
  • 6The roof's back half, and the chimney. The roof carries on 2 m back over the hut's back wall. All of it is green moss, shaggy at the eaves, with nothing orange showing: it is the colour of the garden's floors, which you stand on. A crooked chimney of hollow root comes out of the back half, behind the play space. (1.2 to 3.2 m behind you)
  • 7The frog and its things. The frog, 0.3 m, sits on a toadstool stool against the back wall. It breathes, blinks, watches you and croaks now and then. Beside it are a shelf with three jars and a small clay stove whose pipe is the chimney. A friendly thing, and nothing more (note 41). (1.3 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 8The lantern by the door. A spore pod on a hook of root at the west end of the back wall, glowing warm. It is the one warm light on this floor and you can see it from the snail wall. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 9The root arm's root. A knee of root on the lip that the root arm grows up from, with the root running on back into the moss. (1.2 to 2.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 10The roof: solid. The old stalk's cap, 4.8 m across, under a quilt of moss. It is a real thing in the play space (Parker, 11-13): a low dome you can land on and stand on. Its top is 3.9 m above the floor and nearly flat across the middle. Its eaves hang out 0.9 m past each end of the room and come down to 2.1 m above the floor, so you can reach one from outside the door and pull yourself up. It is hollow underneath: inside the room the ceiling is 2.6 m up, over your head.
  • 11The rug. A round mat of woven mycelium on the garden's floor, 2.6 m across. It is the room's floor: there is no step and no sill.
  • 12Stepping stones. Five flat pebbles set in the moss from the root arm to the door, level with the floor.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 13Fern. One clump at the east corner, in front of the floor's rock face and below where you walk. Nothing stands in front of the hut: it has no front wall. (1.3 to 1.9 m in front)
