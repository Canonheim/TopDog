El Jardín de Hongos · Area and background design

Challenge 4.2: Lianas

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Over your head is the underside of the island: a spur of the mountain that sticks out of the back wall into the middle of the room, black with slime. Water seeps through it along four cracks, and where each crack opens in its belly a creeper has taken root and let one long vine down. Under the vines the floor tips up into a ramp of slime that runs all the way back to the floor of Sombreros. So this is a wet cave roof hung with creeper, over a chute. The four vines are the creeper's nearest strands, 5 m apart because the cracks are.

What must not change

  • The four vines, 5 m apart, each 9.5 m long, hanging from the island's underside.
  • The shelf you leave from and the shelf you land on.
  • The slime ramp under the last two vines, which slides a fall back to the floor of Sombreros.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The forest, far back. One far stalk goes up behind the island, and one whole far mushroom stands on the back of the ramp. Dim and blue-green, 6 to 10 m behind you. (6.0 to 10.0 m behind you)
  • 2The island's belly, going back. The roof you hang under carries on 6 m back, and from its far part hangs the rest of the creeper as one thick, dim mass. It is far enough back, and solid enough, that nobody takes it for a fifth vine. (4.0 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 3A snail, asleep on the slope. Far back on the ramp, stuck where it stopped. (6.0 to 7.0 m behind you)
  • 4Four leaf collars. Where each crack opens in the roof, just behind each vine: a clump of leaves 1.8 m wide with a root as thick as an arm running back along the crack. Each vine is seen to grow out of one. (1.2 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 5The two shelves' stalks. The shelf you leave stands on its thick stalk from the floor. The shelf you land on stands on a shorter one that roots in the ramp behind it. (1.2 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 6Glow from below. Glow moss along the back of the floor, a lantern pod on the first shelf's stalk, and one spore pod glowing on the ramp. The vines are lit from underneath and show pale against the dark roof. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7Seep cracks in the roof. The island's face is a blank black sheet today. It gets four cracks drawn on it, one rising from each vine, with a line of moss in each and slime running from it.
  • 8Vine knots. Each vine comes out of its crack in a knot with two leaves on it, so its top end is not just a line that stops at the rock.
  • 9The landing shelf: an old cap turned up. Cream and dry, gills on top, the same piece as the shelf you left.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 10Fern. One clump at the bottom left, in front of the floor's rock face and below where you walk. (1.3 to 1.9 m in front)
