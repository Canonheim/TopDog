Los Riscos del Viento · Area and background design

Challenge 7.9: Chimenea de Roca

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The top of the tower is split. An upright joint runs through it from west to east, and the east half has slipped down along the joint by a little over a metre, leaving a chimney 2.2 m wide between two fins of rock. Rain runs down both walls, so they are dark and polished. But the tower is made of level beds, and its three hardest beds stand out of each wall as a white band 0.4 m tall. Because the east half has slipped, the same bed is a metre lower on the east wall than on the west, and the bands come in turn: east, west, east, west, east. You go up wall to wall. The chimney has no floor. Under it the shaft drops 10 m into the upper window. At the top you come out on the west fin, and the first painted timber of the cableway is standing on it.

What must not change

  • The two walls, 2.2 m apart, and the five bands, each where it is.
  • The tooth over the porch's inner end, with the first band on it.
  • Five lunges of 2.2 m across and 1.0 to 1.4 m up. No floor: a fall goes down the shaft into the upper window.
  • The west fin's top, where you come out.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The tower's body. The tower's body, a metre proud of the tower on both sides, with the east fin's peak standing behind the peak you see. (1.5 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 2The split's back wall. The chimney's back wall, 1.3 m behind you, is where you see what happened to the tower. The joint runs up its middle as a dark line. The three hard beds cross it as three pale bands, and each band steps down by a metre at the joint. Every band you hold is the end of one of these. (1.3 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 3The pylon head, over the fin. On the west fin's top stands the cableway's low pylon head: a frame of red painted timber with two sheaves on it. From the chimney you see its top over the fin, with the sky behind. It is drawn on the page for La Estación Baja. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 4Seen from here. Below, in the upper window: the vulture's nest and the flag. Beside you, on the porch: the wind sock. All three are on the page for Viento a Favor. (1.3 to 2.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 5The five bands. Each band is the edge of a hard bed: dry white chalk, standing 0.24 m out of the dark wall, with a flat top and a row of small flints under it. They are the only pale things in the chimney.
  • 6The walls. Dark brown, rain-streaked and glossy, with the streaks running straight down. Nothing pale is drawn on them between the bands.
  • 7The west fin's top. The top of the third hard bed, on the west side: 1.8 m of dry white chalk, level. It is the last chalk you climb. The next step is a plank.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • A thin run of water down each wall, and a drip from each band.
  • The wind is loud outside the porch and stops as you step under the tooth.
  • A strip of sky straight overhead between the two fins, with a gondola crossing it now and then.
