Los Riscos del Viento · Area and background design

Challenge 7.10: La Estación Baja

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The chalk ends here and the town's work begins. Above the tower the face is loose scree that nothing can be fixed to, so the cableway's builders stood their low station out from the cliff on a timber scaffold, 24 m wide and 25 m tall. It is the first sawn and painted timber since the town: square posts in yellow ochre, braces in the cableway's red, an iron plate with the pug's face at every joint. Its feet are on the two needles and on the tower's balcony. Its lowest level is the pack trail's old catwalk, in bare honey planks, and that is where a fall puts you. The way up is the machine itself. From the tower's west fin a plank platform with a board missing leads to the bull wheel: a red wheel 7.6 m across with cream spokes, turning once in 24 seconds because the haul cable runs round a drum on its axle. You ride its rim over the top, step off onto a stage, and climb the scaffold's long brace to the station's deck. The brace is rotten in the middle.

What must not change

  • The platform's two lengths and the 1.4 m gap where a board is missing.
  • The bull wheel: where it is, 7.6 m across, one turn in 24 seconds, its east side rising. Rim, spokes and hub can all be held.
  • The stage, and the long brace: 12 m at 56 degrees, with 2.6 m of it rotten, giving way after 1.2 seconds.
  • The old catwalk, 9 to 14 m below, which catches a fall. Its east end drops to the tower's balcony.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The mountain's shoulder. The kit's flat cut-out card of the same cliff, 12 m back, hazed to a mid blue-grey, with ledges and one small dead tree cut into its edge. It stands on the west of the frame only, so the face goes on behind you and never stops inside the frame. East of its edge there is nothing of ours: sky, and the far view that is given. The sketch shows it as a dashed outline. Here it is a strip at the west edge of the frame, seen through the scaffold's west bay. (12.0 to 14.0 m behind you)
  • 2The scaffold. The kit's scaffold frame, moved back out of the play space: four posts 0.35 m square in yellow ochre, a ledger under every plank you stand on, red braces crossed in the bays, and a pug plate at every joint. No brace crosses behind the wheel or behind the long brace. Under the catwalk the four posts go on down as struts to the needles and the balcony. Each plank deck is the near edge of a floor that goes back 1.4 m onto its ledger. (1.4 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 3The wheel's backboard, drum and axle. The wheel's bay is closed at the back with plain ochre boards, 10 m square, so the red rim reads against one flat colour. In front of the boards, on the same axle as the wheel and 2 m behind it, is a plain grey drum 5 m across. The haul cable runs round the drum. That is why the wheel turns. The axle sits in an iron bearing on the scaffold's third post, under a pug plate. (1.8 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 4The haul cable. Black, greased, 0.15 m thick and taut, 2 m behind you: it is not taken for a rope. It comes down from the station's wheel, over one sheave of the pylon head, round the drum, back over the other sheave and up again. Both long runs pass at least 6 m over the stage and the brace, and the two short ones at least 2.5 m over the platform. (1.9 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 5The pylon head. The kit's low pylon head, moved back out of the play space, standing on the body of the tower's west fin: a red timber frame 8 m wide and 5 m tall with two grey sheaves on its top. The platform's planks lie on its lowest rail. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 6The old catwalk's back. Along the back of the catwalk: the trail's handrail of lashed poles, 1.1 m high, unpainted, with one rag of bunting. Against it is a stack of ochre posts that were carried up and never used. It is the last of the trail. (1.4 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 7The low station. The station house, at the top west corner. It is on the page for the join. (2.5 to 5.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The bull wheel. Note 76. The kit's wheel is made again at the course's size, 7.6 m across, so the red rim that is drawn is exactly the rim you hold, and nothing solid lies outside it. Six cream spokes, and the pug on the hub.
  • 9The long brace. It is one of the scaffold's own braces, 0.3 m thick, in the cableway's red, matte, worn to bare honey wood along its top where hands have gone. The rotten 2.6 m wears the cliffs' breakable look: the paint is gone, the wood is grey and split along the grain, and a corner is missing. It creaks and dips before it goes, and it goes in splinters.
  • 10The missing board. In the 1.4 m gap you see the platform's two bearers with nail holes in them, and the board itself lying on the catwalk below.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 11One front post. The scaffold's front west post, 0.3 m thick, from the catwalk's level up to the underside of the station's deck. It stands at the west edge of the frame, in front of air that nobody climbs through, 1.4 m clear of the brace's head. (1.2 to 1.6 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • The cable runs. You see it move round the drum and over the sheaves, and the wheel turns with it.
  • The whole scaffold ticks and creaks, and the catwalk's planks knock when you land on them.
  • The daylight has started to turn gold on the scaffold's west side.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 76 (cause found, not fixed): the wheel that is drawn and the wheel you touch become the same size. The model is made again at the course's 7.6 m, so no reach changes.
  • Scenery moved out of the play space, where it stands today: the scaffold's frame and the pylon head.
  • The cableway's own towers (biome 8's) stand in this picture today as flat red cards, from the catwalk's height upward. They belong to that biome's pages and are not drawn here.
  • The cable's road is chosen to keep it away from every place you stand. No part of it comes within 2.5 m of a plank or within 6 m of the brace.
  • The scaffold's four legs, down to the needles and the balcony, are new. They are asked about on the page for Los Burros, because that is where they show.
  • Nothing you touch moves or is added.
