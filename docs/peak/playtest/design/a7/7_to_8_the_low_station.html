Los Riscos del Viento · Area and background design

Join 7 to 8: The low station: from the cliffs to the cableway

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The top of the cliffs, 688 m up, and the bottom of the cableway. The long brace brings you up over the east end of a deck of honey planks 14 m long. Its west end rests on the last white chalk of the face. Its east end is carried by the scaffold. Behind the deck, 2.5 m back, is the town's low station house: board walls, red below and cream above, a striped tin roof, dark windows, a cold lantern by the door. Nobody is here. Within this one screen everything changes hands. Chalk gives way to scree above you. Lashed poles and hemp give way to sawn timber and paint, and then to steel and cable. The gusts that pushed you about on the cliffs become one steady wind, and the sock on the deck's back corner points east and stays there. The light has turned from blue toward gold. At the deck's east end is the boarding gangway, and every six seconds a car comes down to it. From there on it is biome 8.

What must not change

  • The deck: 14 m long, its top at 688 m, and the plug of chalk at its west end.
  • The head of the long brace, at the deck's east end.
  • Biome 8's gangway on the deck's east end, 0.7 m higher, with its one rail post, and the cars that come down to it.
  • The face above the deck: slick scree with nothing on it.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The mountain's shoulder. The cliffs' flat cut-out card, 12 m back, carried on up behind the station house. Above the deck's height its chalk gives way to dark scree, as biome 8's sheet has it. (12.0 to 14.0 m behind you)
  • 2The station house. The kit's low station house, 14 m wide and 6 m tall, moved back to stand 2.5 m behind you, with no roof and no post over the deck. So nothing within reach looks like something to hold (note 77). The deck's planks carry on back to its door. Its ticket booth, its pug sign and its wind engine are biome 8's and are on that biome's pages. (2.5 to 5.5 m behind you)
  • 3The cold lantern. The lantern that stands on the deck today, moved back to hang on a bracket beside the house's door. It is not lit. The last lit lantern was in the tea cave. (2.2 to 2.5 m behind you)
  • 4The wind sock. On a post 3 m tall at the deck's back west corner: the same red and white sock as on the tower's porch. Here it points east and stays there, full. It is the first thing that tells you the wind has changed its ways. (2.1 to 2.5 m behind you)
  • 5The line of pennants. The kit's pennant line, 26 m of the town's bunting, from the house's east gable out to a pole on the scaffold's far corner. Today it hangs at head height along the deck, inside the play space. It goes 2 m back and up to 5 m above the deck, over every hand. All its pennants point east. (2.0 to 2.3 m behind you)
  • 6Under the deck. What carries the deck: a ledger under its planks, the scaffold's west post under its east end, and two raking struts in yellow ochre from the ledger down to iron shoes in the face. A pug plate is at each strut's head. (1.4 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 7The haul cable, going up. The two runs of the cliffs' haul cable come up from the bull wheel, 2 m behind you, and go round the wheel over the dock. That wheel is biome 8's, a flat greyed bull wheel 4.4 m across, and the station's wind engine turns it. So the wheel you rode below is turned from here. (1.9 to 2.2 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The last chalk. The plug at the deck's west end is the cliffs' last white chalk: dry, matte, with a grass tuft on it. The deck's first plank is cut to fit against it. Above it the face is dark scree.
  • 9The deck's planks. Honey planks laid across the deck, 0.3 m wide, with two rows of nail heads. They are the same planks as the old catwalk's, but new, sawn square and nailed, not lashed. The joints are drawn on the deck that is there.
  • 10The brace's head. The long brace is bolted to the deck's end beam through an iron plate with the pug's face on it. The plate is the last of the scaffold.
  • 11Where the cableway begins. The step up onto the gangway. The deck's planks are bare. The gangway's edge is painted with the cableway's cream band, the first of that biome's marks for a hold. One step, and the paint changes.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 12One scaffold post. One painted post, 0.3 m thick, in front of the deck's west end, from below the frame up to just under the deck's top. It stands in front of the air under the deck, where nobody is. Biome 8's sheet counts on it. (1.2 to 1.6 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • The wind sock and the pennants point east without a break. No wavy lines here: this wind does not push you (biome 8's sheet).
  • Gold light on the house's west wall, and long shadows across the deck.
  • A car comes down to the gangway every six seconds. You hear its grip rattle on the cable before you are over the deck's edge.
