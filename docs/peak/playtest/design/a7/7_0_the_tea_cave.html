Los Riscos del Viento · Area and background design

Rest 7.0: The tea cave

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The drivers' shelter. When the trail worked, a burro driver caught on the gully by a rockfall got into the upper pocket and waited. Somebody got tired of waiting in the wind, and dug the pocket's back wall out into a room: 5 m wide, 2.4 m tall, in the solid rock under the first burro's ledge. The drivers are gone. The frog lives in it now, and keeps a pot of tea going. It sits at a low table with a cup, and a second cup is set out for you. Outside is wind, falling rock and the whole mountain's drop. In here the wind's sound falls away, the light is amber from a small lantern and a brazier, and nothing can hurt you. The room is cut away at the front: floor, back wall and roof. It leads nowhere.

What must not change

  • The upper pocket, its step, and the lip's nose above it: the route up the gully is the same.
  • The boulders still jump the pocket. None can come into the room.
  • The first burro's ledge above, and the rock between.
  • The only new things you touch are the doorway and the room's floor, both from note 68.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The gully's far wall. The arch's body behind the gully, as on the page for Desprendimiento. (1.5 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 2The room. One snug room dug in the rock, 5 m wide, 2.4 m tall and 2.2 m deep behind the line you walk. Its walls are pale chalk with the pick marks still on them, lit amber. The roof is black with smoke over the brazier. (1.2 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 3The rug and two cushions. A woven rug in the town's colours, 2.6 m long, on the back half of the floor, with a cushion at each end of it. (1.4 to 2.9 m behind you)
  • 4The table, the pot and two cups. A low table, 0.8 m wide, in the middle of the rug. On it are a pot that steams and two cups. The cup at the east end, nearest the door, is yours. (1.7 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 5The frog. 0.3 m tall, sitting on the west cushion with its cup. It blinks at you, and now and then it sips (notes 68 and 60). (1.9 to 2.3 m behind you)
  • 6The shelf and the pegs. On the back wall: a shelf cut into the rock with a row of tins and cups, and beside it three iron pegs with a pack saddle and a folded burro blanket hanging on them. The pegs say whose room this was. (3.1 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 7The brazier, the kettle and the crack. A small iron brazier on three legs by the door, with a kettle on it and orange coals under. A thread of smoke goes up into a crack in the roof. The crack comes out on the gully's top tread, between two knobs, and the smoke is the first sign of the room that you see from outside. (1.6 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 8The lantern. A small lantern on a peg under the roof at the back. With the brazier it is the only warm light on the cliffs. (3.0 to 3.2 m behind you)
  • 9The door blanket. A striped burro blanket, hung as a door curtain and tied back to a peg on the far side of the doorway. From the gully it is the one patch of bright colour in the rock, and it is how you find the door. (1.2 to 1.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 10The doorway. New, from note 68. The upper pocket's back wall is opened from floor to roof: a doorway 2.2 m tall. Its jambs are dry white chalk.
  • 11The room's floor. New, from note 68. 5 m of floor, level with the pocket's floor, in dry white chalk worn smooth. You walk in and stand anywhere on it.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 12The cut edge. A low lip of rock along the front edge of the floor, below the floor's top, to show that the room's front was taken away. It covers nothing. (1.2 to 1.5 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • Steam from the pot, and the thread of smoke from the brazier into the crack and out on the tread above.
  • Warm light on the pocket's floor outside the door.
  • Inside, the wind's sound falls away. You hear the kettle, and the boulders going over as a far-off knocking.
  • The frog croaks when you come within a screen of the door (note 60).

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 68 (settled, not built): the little cave with a tea corner and a frog. The doorway is where the note's picture has the climber, in the upper pocket, and the room runs back into the rock the note circles.
  • Note 68 also saw a pale block covered in dark patches in that rock. It is the first burro's ledge: the course gives the ledge a block of holdable rock 8 m deep, and it lies inside the dark rock of the face. The room is cut through both.
  • Note 60: this is the cliffs' frog in a room. The second frog is in the vulture's nest (Nidos).
  • Course change, from note 68: a doorway 2.2 m tall is cut through the upper pocket's back wall, and behind it are 5 m of new floor to stand on. Nothing else you touch moves.
