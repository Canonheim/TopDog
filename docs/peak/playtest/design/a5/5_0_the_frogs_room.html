Rest 5.0: The frog's room

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

A small side shrine, cut into the west wall at the ledge you reach after El Contrapeso, at the foot of the serpent's tail. Every hall of the temple had shrines like it, where a climber left a small carved pug before a test. This one is 2.8 m deep and 2.8 m tall inside, with a stepped doorway 2 m tall. Its walls are shelves of carved pugs the size of a hand, dozens of them. On the offering table at the back, in the place of honour where the biggest pug should sit, sits the frog. The room is cut away at the front: you see its floor, its back wall and its roof.

What must not change

  • The ledge and the tail chain stay as they are. The room adds 2.8 m of level floor west of the ledge and nothing else.
  • Nothing in the room can be held or stood on except its floor. The table and the shelves are behind you.
  • Finding the frog is never needed to climb on (note 60).

The three layers

Background

  • 1The west wall, behind the ledge. The same back wall as the scale's. The torch over the ledge is the one light outside the room. (2.6 to 3.2 m behind you)
  • 2The shrine. The room, cut into rock that is solid today. Fitted pale blocks, a stepped roof, and a back wall 2.4 m behind you. The wall here is 3 m thick today, so the rock behind the room has to be made a metre thicker when this is built. (1.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 3Shelves of small carved pugs. Two stone shelves round the back wall, crowded with carved pugs a hand tall, each a little different, some with a jade bead or a scrap of cut paper. They are the offerings of everyone who came this far. (2.0 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 4The offering table, and the frog. A stone table 1.2 m wide against the back wall, under an empty stepped niche where the shrine's own carved pug should be. The frog, 0.3 m, sits on the table in front of the niche, facing out. It breathes, blinks and watches you. Two small lamps burn either side of it. (1.4 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 5The scale's west pan and the serpent's bed. What else is on this wall, for placing the room: the west hanger slot of the scale, and the bed of the serpent overhead with the tail's boss. (1.3 to 2.2 m behind you)

Play space

  • 6A line of jade paw prints. Four small paw prints of jade, inlaid in the top of the ledge, lead to the doorway. They are the only sign from outside, with the frog's croak.
  • 7The doorway. A stepped opening in the wall's face, 2 m tall at the ledge and 2.8 m inside. Its frame is carved pale. The wall above and below it stays polished black.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
