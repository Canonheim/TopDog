El Templo del Primer Perro · Area and background design

Challenge 5.6: Incensarios

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The east end of the great hall, over the drain. This is where the temple burned its incense: one great censer hangs on a bronze chain from a carved pug in the roof and swings out over the place where the ramp ends and the hall drops 38 m to the entry floor. Across the gap is the keeper's balcony, a small ledge on the well's wall where the censer was filled, with a doorway behind it and a chain up to the gallery. The censer still swings and still smokes. Nobody fills it.

What must not change

  • After note 50 there is one censer, the middle one: where it hangs, its 7.8 m chain, its swing of 1.6 m either side, once every 5 seconds.
  • The fling from the last champion's fist to the censer, and from the censer to the balcony.
  • The drain under it, the keeper's balcony, and the chain up to the gallery.
  • Nothing hangs near the censer, in front of it or behind it.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The hall's far wall, carried on to the well's wall. The same bays as the rest of the hall. They carry on east behind the balcony and up behind the chain, to the well's wall, so there is no dark band there (note 51). Behind the censer's swing the wall is left plain, with no carved band, so the gold bowl and its smoke show against bare stone. (9.0 to 10.0 m behind you)
  • 2The last pilaster. The third champion's pilaster, 16 m of it, rising from the ramp. It is the last solid thing before the drain. (1.3 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 3The fall strip. The carved strip of tumbling bones starts here, at the height of the censer, and runs down the far wall behind the drain's mouth and on down. It is the same strip you walked past at the door. A stone pug's face looks down the drain from beside it. (8.6 to 9.0 m behind you)
  • 4The keeper's doorway. A stepped arch doorway behind the balcony, dark inside, with two incense jars and a long ladle beside it. The chain you climb hangs in front of it. (1.3 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 5Two chain bosses. A carved pug with a bronze ring in its mouth for each chain: one under the roof over the censer, one under the stone above the shaft for the chain to the gallery. Each is set just behind the play space and reaches its ring forward, so the boss is scenery and the chain is what you hold. (1.3 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 6The gallery's columns. Above the roof: the gallery you will walk west along, with its square columns and its back wall. (2.4 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 7Torches. One beside the last champion, one either side of the censer's swing and well clear of it, one in the chain's shaft, and one low down beside the drain so the drop is lit. (8.7 to 9.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The one censer. A pierced bronze bowl with a gold lid, coals glowing inside, and a jade band round it because it moves. Its chain is thick bronze, link by link, and can be held along its whole length, up to the ring it hangs from.
  • 9The two that go. The first and third censers are in the course today and are crossed out here. They go, with their chains and their bosses (note 50). The banner that hangs beside the first chain today goes too.
  • 10The balcony's edge. The west edge of the keeper's balcony is carved deep and pale. It is what you fling for.
  • 11The ramp's foot. The ramp ends in a lip of polished black, worn into a shallow spout.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
