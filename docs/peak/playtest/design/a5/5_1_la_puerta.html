El Templo del Primer Perro · Area and background design

Challenge 5.1: La Puerta

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

This is the temple's front door and the hall inside it. You come through a doorway 16 m tall, cut through the well's wall, onto 33 m of fitted paving. At the far end the floor meets the great stepped platform that the First Top Dog's mask sits on: five tiers, each 2.5 m high and 3 m deep, built for someone much bigger than you. The tiers are the first test. Three of them are false: each is a slab on bronze shoes in a slot, and when weight comes onto it, it draws back under the tier above. Under the platform runs the chute, a slope of polished black stone. The temple was built with a way back down from every test, and they all end on this floor.

What must not change

  • The five tiers and the landing, each exactly where it is, and which three slide: the second, the fourth and the fifth.
  • How a false tier moves: 2.6 m west under the tier above, half gone in a second, back after 4 seconds.
  • The chute under the upper tiers, and the open floor in front of the door.
  • Nothing stands in front of the tiers or the floor above the level of the paving.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The entry hall's back wall. Wall bays of pale fitted blocks, 4 m wide and 5 m tall, one row above another, with the carved band between the rows. It runs the whole width of the frame and out of its top, so there is no black behind anything (note 51). It is 6 m back, in brown shadow. (6.0 to 7.0 m behind you)
  • 2Two doorways. Two stepped arch doorways in the back wall, dark inside, each with a small stone pug plaque over it and a cut-paper banner high above. The hall goes on behind you. Today one mask 6 m across hangs on this wall. It goes: the first big face you see should be the great mask at the top of the tiers. (6.0 to 7.0 m behind you)
  • 3The fall strip. A carved strip 5 m wide runs up the back wall and out of the frame, behind the place where the great hall's drain comes down. Small carved figures fall down it, head first, all the way from the top. You walk past it on your way in, before you know what it is for. (5.6 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 4The platform the mask sits on. The body of the stepped platform, right behind the tiers you climb. Each tier you touch is the front edge of a terrace that runs 4 m back into the scene. On the platform's face under each tier, a carved band shows small figures climbing the tiers, and one of them being scraped off. (1.3 to 5.5 m behind you)
  • 5Three slots. Each false tier rides in a slot cut along the platform's face and lined with bronze. The slot is 5.7 m long: the 3 m the tier fills, and 2.6 m of empty channel to the west of it. The empty channel shows you, before you step, that this tier moves and how far it goes. The true tiers have no slot. (1.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 6The door through the well's wall. The side of the doorway: the same pale blocks, with the wall stepping in above your head. The mushroom garden has reached this far: thick roots lie in the joints of the first few blocks, and three small orange mushrooms grow at the foot of the wall. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 7Torches. Four on the back wall along the floor and one on the platform's face. The floor and the tiers are warm and clear, and the wall above them falls away into brown. (5.7 to 6.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8A jade band and a bronze shoe on each false tier. The temple's mark for a thing that moves. Each false tier has a jade stripe down its front face and bronze along its bottom edge. It shakes and grinds for a moment before it goes.
  • 9Deep carving on the true tiers. The front faces of the first and third tiers and the landing are carved deep and pale, with pug faces and belts. They are bonded into the platform, with no joint under them.
  • 10Polish and wear. Drawn onto what is there. The chute is polished black with pale runs down it. Where the drain lands, the paving is worn into a shallow dish 5 m across, scuffed pale.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 11A low balustrade. Along the front edge of the paving, 0.8 m tall. It stands a step down from the floor, so its top is below the level you walk on and it covers no part of you. It stops before the first tier. (1.3 to 1.6 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • A false tier shakes and drops a line of dust from its slot before it draws back.
  • A puff of dust on the worn dish whenever something lands from the drain.
  • A few of the garden's spores drift in through the door and die out by the first torch.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 51: the back wall runs the whole frame. There is no black behind the floor or the tiers.
  • Note 48, the part about these steps: they slide into open air under the next tier, not into a wall, so nothing here pins a hand. The slot is in the platform behind them.
