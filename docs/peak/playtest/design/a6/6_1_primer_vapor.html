La Chimenea · Area and background design

Challenge 6.1: Primer Vapor

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The forge's door and its first breath. The temple's smiths found a vent here, in the floor at the foot of the flue, where the mountain blows steam every few seconds, and they built their forge round it. They put a grate over the vent and cast a face for it: an iron pug as tall as a house, fixed to the back wall, breathing the vent's steam. The ring in its nose, the chain on the ring and the tongue hanging out of its mouth are what you hold. The light: you come in from the temple's torch gold. One last torch burns at the door. Two steps on, the light is furnace red and comes from below, up through the grating, and from the great furnace to the east. This is the red end of the journey.

What must not change

  • The grate and its vent, the ring, the chain and the iron ledge, each exactly where it is.
  • Note 63: the vent is about 10% stronger. Nothing in the design depends on how high it throws.
  • Nothing stands in front of the vent.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The forge's back wall. The same wall as the temple across the well: big fitted blocks, a sloped base, the carved band at the temple's own heights. Here it is black with soot and the carving is worn nearly away. It fills the frame from the door to the great furnace. (3.6 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 2The door, a small pug over it, and the last torch. The stepped arch you came through, seen from inside, with a small iron pug mask over it. One torch burns beside it. It is the last of the temple's torches: east of it the light is red and comes from below. (1.3 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 3The great iron mask. An iron pug's face 8 m wide and 11 m tall, cast in plates and riveted, fixed to a pier of the back wall with its chin 5 m above the floor. It is the forge's own copy of the temple's great mask. It is redrawn so that what you hold belongs to it: the ring is in its nose, the chain hangs from the ring down over its mouth, and its tongue lolls out of the east corner of its mouth and flattens into the plate you land on. It is black iron, set back, and it can't be mistaken for a wall to climb. (1.3 to 1.9 m behind you)
  • 4The breath pipe. A fat ribbed pipe comes up out of the floor behind the grate and goes into the mask's chin. The grate is the open end of the same steam, so when the vent blows the mask breathes out through its nostrils and its mouth as well. Wisps come from the nostrils first, then the blast. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 5The strip of grating, with the fire under it. The grate you stand on is the front bay of a strip of grating that runs back across the floor to the foot of the pipe. Red light comes up through all of it. This is where the red light from below takes over from the torches. (1.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 6Anvil, tongs rack and quenching trough by the door. The work place nearest the door: the small anvil that stands here today, a rack of tongs on the wall over it, and a stone trough. The rack hangs 3 m up the west wall today. It comes down to where a smith could reach it. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 7The great furnace's corner. The west corner of the furnace of Piedra Caliente, 1 m east of the tongue's tip. Its fire is the red on the right side of everything here. (1.3 to 3.2 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The landing slab. The ledge the censer drops you on is temple stone, soot-black on its face and worn pale on top, where it can be held. Its edge carries the temple's carved band one last time.
  • 9The grate's rim. The kit's steam grate: square iron bars in a raised rim, rust orange where feet have worn it. Every vent on the climb wears this same grate, so after this one you know what a grate does.
  • 10The tongue's rolled tip. The plate you land on is the mask's tongue: riveted iron, rust orange on top, curled under at its east tip. The curl points at the furnace, where the climb goes next.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 11The floor's kerb. A low lip of soot-black blocks along the very bottom of the frame, below the floor. The same kerb runs the whole length of the forge floor. It covers nothing you can stand on. (1.3 to 2.0 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • Wisps from the mask's nostrils and a brighter red in the grate's bars for the 1.2 s before each blast, with the gurgle.
  • The torch's flame leans into the hall: the forge draws air in through the door.
  • Embers rising slowly through the whole frame.
