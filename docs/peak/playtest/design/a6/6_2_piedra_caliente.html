La Chimenea · Area and background design

Challenge 6.2: Piedra Caliente

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The forge's great furnace. It is a tower of soot-black temple blocks standing on the floor, 9 m wide, stepping back three times as it rises, the way every temple wall does. It has burned for so long that its face has cracked, and the climb goes up the crack. The hot holds are the ends of through stones: long blocks laid right through the wall with their far ends in the fire. The three cool holds are on the furnace's three carved bands, which stand clear of the fire. The ledge at the top is its charging step. The light: the deepest red on the climb. It comes from the furnace's mouth, straight below you, and from the hot stones themselves. Everything above the top band is black.

What must not change

  • The twelve holds, each exactly where it is: nine hot, three cool.
  • Note 56, already built: the face is burnt black along the line of the holds, and the hot holds pulse, steam and show their time running out.
  • Nothing under the climb but the floor.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The forge's back wall. Black temple blocks with the worn band, running on behind the furnace on both sides. (3.6 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 2The iron mask's cheek. From Primer Vapor: the east side of the great mask. The iron ledge you start on is its tongue. (1.3 to 1.9 m behind you)
  • 3The great furnace. Four tiers of temple blocks, 9.2 m wide at the foot and 20 m tall. A carved band runs round the top of each of the first three tiers, 490.0, 493.6 and 496.1 m up. The iron tongue you start on stops 1 m short of its west corner. The ledge at the top is its charging step, carried on a stone corbel, and its crown is open and glowing. (1.3 to 3.2 m behind you)
  • 4The furnace mouth and its hood. Low in the furnace's face, straight below the climb: a stepped arch 3 m wide with the fire in it, under a ribbed iron hood. This is the light of the challenge. It is deep red and it comes from below, so the undersides of the holds are lit. (1.3 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 5The crack. A heat crack runs up the face along the line of the holds, and the stone is burnt black for a metre either side of it (note 56, built). The fire shows in the crack as a thin red line. (1.3 to 1.4 m behind you)
  • 6The air pipe. A ribbed pipe comes out of the wall above the furnace and down into its crown. It carries the air from the three small bellows of El Fuelle, 13 m overhead. The furnace is what all the bellows are for. (2.0 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 7Coal heap and rake. On the floor by the mouth: a heap of coal, and a long iron rake leaning on the furnace. (1.5 to 2.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The three cool bosses. Each cool hold sits exactly on one of the furnace's three carved bands. It is the one boss of the band's carving that has not worn away: a small pug's head in grey stone, plainly cold, with a wisp of steam where damp runs along the band onto it.
  • 9The nine hot stones. Each hot hold is the square end of a through stone, and it glows, pulses and steams as the game shows it today. The joint round it glows too, so you can see it is a different stone from the wall.
  • 10The charging step. The ledge at the top is a slab of temple stone with a worn carved edge. The grate of the next challenge is set in it.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 11The floor's kerb. A low lip of soot-black blocks along the very bottom of the frame, below the floor. The same kerb runs the whole length of the forge floor. It covers nothing you can stand on. (1.3 to 2.0 m in front)
