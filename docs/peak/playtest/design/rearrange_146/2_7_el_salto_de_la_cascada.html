Challenge 2.7: El Salto de la Cascada (was El Charco del Pozo)

Design only. Nothing here is built. Numbers marked first guess are to be tuned by playing it.

The place, in one paragraph

The foot of El Pozo del Diablo, the brick hall round the old reservoir, as today: but the great falls now come down the well and straight through the hall. Where they hit, the reservoir's floor has broken, and they pour on down through the hole. The pool is cut in two. Its west half, fed by the flume from 2.8, runs east and pours over a sill into the falls. Its east half is a basin under the ledge you start from. From the ledge's west end you swing out on the barrel men's old davit chain and let go, over the falling water, into the west half, and the moment you land you have to swim, because it is taking you back to the falls. Behind the spray, the well goes up out of sight; under you, the falls go down out of sight.

What must not change about the play

  • The start on Q6 (the east ledge), the frog's door at its east end, and the step down from 2.6's gallery.
  • Q7 (the west ledge) and 2.8's start beyond it; the flume from 2.8 spilling into the pool's west end.
  • The well's mouth in the roof, x -306 to -294, and the camera's lean upward.
  • The pool's current, 1.0 m a second, and two swims of about 4 m against it.
  • The old cascade and the black waters' lower river, now fed from the east basin.
  • A miss costs what it cost before: down to the Sump (today by the lip, the cascade, the river and the mine; now by the falls).

The four layers

1. The painted backdrop

far, 12 m and more behind: one painted scene, never a flat colour

  • 1The view down through the floor. Under the hall's broken floor: a painted card of the falls dropping away into the dark, mist rising, and far below the warm dots of the mine's lanterns. What a miss costs, shown before you jump (law 5). (12.0 to 16.0 m behind you)
  • 2The look up the well. As today: five rings of black rock cut out of card, each smaller, further back and higher, and the dot of daylight at the top. Now the great falls come down out of it, pale against the rings. (2.0 to 8.0 m behind you)

2. The painted set

the near back wall and its depth, 2 to 10 m behind

  • 3The hall's two brick flanks. As today: brick, 15 m tall, west and east of the well, each ending in a brick pier against the rock, with the bricked-up arch in the east one. (5.5 to 6.5 m behind you)
  • 4The broken floor. Where the falls hit, the reservoir's floor has broken through: a 6 m hole with the brick lining hanging in ragged edges over the drop, the pool's west half pouring over a worn sill into it and the east basin's edge cracked. The falls go through, down a shaft cut in the black waters' floor, to the new section and the mine. (1.3 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 5The gantry. An iron beam across the pool's west half, 2.6 m behind you, hung from the roof on two flat iron straps: what the barrel store's men slung their hoists from. Its two chains hang forward into your plane. (1.4 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 6The davit. An iron davit on Q6's west end, its arm out over the falls' edge: where the barrel men stood to hook barrels off the old lip. Its chain is the launch. (1.3 to 1.5 m behind you)

3. Real things

the hero piece and the things round the climb

  • 7The great falls. The hero piece: the falls themselves, drawn as 3D water at full width (note 111), coming out of the well and through the hall and its floor, spray and mist filling the gap you leap. They do not push you sideways here (first guess): the leap decides. Whoever goes into them rides them all the way down to the Sump (note 147, settled). (in the play space)
  • 8The lantern on the far bank. A works lantern on a short post at Q7's east end, the brightest thing on the far side: the light to aim at. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 9What has fallen. As today, moved to a shelf at the back of the pool's west half: a broken crystal point, a rotted mushroom cap, a carved pug block, a coil of rope. One thing from each place above where the well is crossed. (3.4 to 5.0 m behind you)

4. The climb as things

every ledge, hold, rope and mover a real object, its drawing the shape of its solid

  • 10Q6, longer. Today's east ledge, carried on 5.6 m west to the falls' edge (x -296.6) on more iron beams out of the east flank. Its west end is the launch. Under it, the east basin still runs to the old cascade and the lower river. (in the play space)
  • 11The davit's chain. 2.2 m of chain from the davit's arm, its tail at y 119.4: take it from Q6, swing, and let go west over the falls. (in the play space)
  • 12The pool's lip. A worn stone sill where the pool's west half pours over into the falls, white water breaking on it. The current quickens to 1.6 m a second over its last 2 m. (in the play space)
  • 13C1 and C2. Two chains from the gantry, their tails 0.6 m over the water: C1 at x -306.8, 3.8 m from the lip (the first grab after the leap, and the last chance for anyone the current takes back toward the falls: law 7); C2 at x -310.6. (in the play space)
  • 14Q7's ladder. Two iron rungs down Q7's east end into the water: the way out, as every way out of moving water in the works is a chain or a ladder. (in the play space)
