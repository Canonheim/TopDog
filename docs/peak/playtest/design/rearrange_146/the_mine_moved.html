La Mina Vieja, moved under the great falls

Design only. Nothing here is built. Numbers marked first guess are to be tuned by playing it.

The place, in one paragraph

The whole mine moves 59 m west and 17 m down, so its waterfall cavern stands straight under the well and the great falls come down through it in one line from under the forge to the Sump. Nothing inside it changes: every challenge, every hold, every lantern and every piece of scenery moves by the same amount. Two things change at its edges. Its west wall moves a further 6 m west, as Parker drew it, so the falls have room. And its top no longer opens into the black waters: La Cortina lets out into the new section's lip room, beside the flume's lip, where Los Barriles starts.

The widened falls area at its foot

Parker's drawing moves the chamber's whole west wall about 6 m further west than the rest of the mine, from the Sump's floor to the throat's top (measured off the drawing: 6.6 m). So: the great falls, which today have 4 m of open air on their west side before the wall, get 10 m; the Sump is 6 m longer at its west end; the frog's cave moves with the wall; the throat is 24 m wide at its top (x -313 to -289), its roof carried on west over the widened part; the mist and the painted cavern fill the space. The falls' slot in the roof stays 6 m (x -303 to -297) and the falls' push stays on it, so La Cortina, the wheel, the awning and the life ring stand to the falls exactly as today. The swim from the falls' foot to the frog's cave grows from 4 m to 9 m, so one more old staging pile stands in it.

Why it drops 17 m too

Moved only west, the throat's roof (y 86 to 90) would sit inside the black waters' rock, which starts at y 83 west of x -292: 110 square metres of overlap. And there would be no room between the mine's top and El Borde (y 90) for the flume and the lock. Dropped 17 m, as in Parker's drawing, its top is at y 73, 10 m under the black waters' lowest rock, and the 17 m to El Borde hold the lip room, the flume and a 12 m lock exactly. The Sump's floor goes to y -50, inside the game's bug net at y -60: a drop of more than about 26 m would not fit.

What must not change about the play

  • Every challenge 1.1 to 1.11 exactly as today, relative to itself: the moves, the rhythms (the cave-in's written rocks, the curtain's drips), the carts, the wheel, the lift.
  • The falls' slot, 6 m, and their push on it; La Cortina's place beside them; the awning and the life ring.
  • The Sump and its swim (6 m longer at the west end, with one more pile to hold).
  • The door from the cart tunnel into 1.1 La Galería.
