La Gruta de Cristal · Area and background design

Challenge 3.4: La Geoda

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

A geode is a hollow stone lined with crystal. This one is 6 m across. The temple's people cut it out of the back wall, sawed a notch in its side, and hung it on a stone axle so that it turns: three times a minute (note 29). Nobody knows what turns it, as nobody knows what turns the temple's machines. It is open towards you like a bowl on its side. You step in through the notch, walk its lining like the inside of a wheel, and step out through the notch when it comes round to the top, beside a broken spar of crystal that leads on. The round hole in the wall that the geode came out of is still there behind it.

What must not change

  • The geode's place and size: 5 m across inside, with one gap 60 degrees wide.
  • It turns the way it turns today, and after note 29 half as fast again: three turns a minute.
  • Its rind can't be held. Its lining can.
  • The spar over its crown, the rest beyond with its glass west lip, and the glass crystal hanging from the roof to the west.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The socket it was cut from. A round hollow in the back wall, 7.5 m across, a little bigger than the geode and straight behind it, like a cupped hand. Its inside is dark and lined with the stumps of points. You see it as a dark ring round the geode. It says the geode grew here and was taken out by somebody. (3.0 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 2The geode's back. The far half of the bowl, close behind you as you walk inside it: the same short points as the lining you stand on, bluer and dimmer, all pointing in at the middle. It turns with the rest. (1.2 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 3The hub: a temple boss on the axle's end. The geode turns on a stone axle that runs back into the wall between two carved stone cheeks. From the front the geode hides them. What you see is the axle's end in the middle of the geode's back: a carved pug boss 0.9 m across with a bronze cap, going round with it. The pug turning over tells you how fast the geode turns and which way. (1.3 to 3.2 m behind you)
  • 4An offering niche. In the back wall above the rest you start from, at head height: a carved hollow with a small crystal and a jade ring, the same as the one at the chandeliers. (2.6 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 5The roots of the two rests and the spar. The rest you leave, the rest you reach and the spar between are the ends of pale crystals from the wall, each with its root fan. The spar is a thin flat one, snapped off short over the geode's crown. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 6The bright crystal over the way out. One bright white cluster on the wall above the spar. The way out is the brightest place in the frame, so your eye goes to it while you wait for the gap to come round. (1.6 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 7A mirror face under the geode. A clear face on the back wall, low and to the east. It shows the geode's underside going round. It is there today, a little higher. (4.3 to 4.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The geode's two skins. Outside, the rind: smooth banded stone in dark blue-grey rings, glossy, which can't be held. Inside, the lining: a crust of short rough points in pale lilac, which can. The front edge is sawn flat and shows both skins as rings, like a cut geode on a shelf.
  • 9The notch's two sawn lips. The gap is not a break: it is cut. Its two ends are flat and straight, with saw marks, so it reads as a doorway somebody made. The lining's pale edge shows on each lip.
  • 10The spar's broken end, and the far rest's wet lip. The spar ends in a rough pale break you can hold all round. The west lip of the rest beyond it is glass under a drip, like the lip above the mirror.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • A faint glow from the lining, so the inside of the geode is lighter than the wall round it.
  • A low grinding from the axle, and a click each time the notch passes the top.
  • A few grains of glitter shaken off the lining as it turns.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 29 (not built yet): the geode turns half as fast again, three turns a minute. The wait from stepping in to the gap reaching the top drops from 22 seconds to about 15.
