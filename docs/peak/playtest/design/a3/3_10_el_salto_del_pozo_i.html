La Gruta de Cristal · Area and background design

Challenge 3.10: El Salto del Pozo I

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The end of the cave, and the way into the mushroom garden. The well, El Pozo del Diablo, runs up through the mountain just east of here, and at this height its wall has split open: a breach 14 m tall, the cave's door. The longest rough crystal in the cave has grown out through it: a horn 16 m long, rising at 20 degrees from the last shelf, with its tip 1.4 m out over the shaft. From here the well is black. Its walls are wet and slick, there is no bottom to see, and far overhead there is one small dot of light. Across it the far wall is broken open too, and that side is not crystal. A thick root has pushed out into the shaft above the far door, and three thinner roots hang from it like a curtain, 9 m from the horn's tip. You go up the horn, fling from its tip, and catch a root. Behind the roots there is moss, a green glow and the first mushroom. The temple's people came into the cave this way, down the well: their last carved pug is over the door.

What must not change

  • The horn: 16 m long, rising 20 degrees, its tip 1.4 m into the well and 7 m above the sill. All of it can be held.
  • The shelf and the sill it leaves from.
  • The well: 12 m wide, slick on both walls, with nothing to catch between the horn's tip and the roots.
  • The far side: the root that sticks 2.5 m out into the well, the three hanging roots 12 m long, and the sill of roots under them.
  • A miss goes down the well.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The well's far wall. The shaft is round, and its far side curves behind the crossing: black rock, wet, with thin white runs of water and no ledges. It is the darkest thing in the cave. Below the frame it goes on down with no floor in sight, only a little mist. The cave's violet reaches one arm's length into it and stops. (6.0 to 8.0 m behind you)
  • 2The dot of light. Far up the shaft, at the top of the frame when the camera pulls back for the jump: one small pale dot, the well's mouth, 210 m above. A faint grey light comes down the middle of the shaft from it and is gone before it reaches you. It is the same dot you saw from the river. (6.0 to 7.0 m behind you)
  • 3The horn's root. The horn's thick end is buried in a collar of dark rock and small points that stands out from the back wall across the shelf. It grew from there towards the well, as if towards the air. The shelf's own fan of points is under it. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 4The breach, carrying on behind. Behind the doorway the well's wall is whole again: the door is a split a little over 2 m deep. On the cave side its broken edges are crusted with small pale points. On the well side they are bare black rock. The line between the two is where the cave ends. (1.3 to 4.0 m behind you)
  • 5The temple's ring over the door. High on the back wall beside the top of the door: a carved stone pug with an empty bronze ring in its mouth, under a skin of clear crystal, and an offering niche next to it. It is where the temple's people made fast when they came down the well. No chain hangs from it now. It is the last temple work in the cave, and the same stone, bronze and jade as the temple two biomes up. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 6The bright crystal at the door. One bright white cluster on the back wall above the horn. It lights the horn from behind, so the horn is a pale spear against the well's black, and it is the last white light before the garden's green. (1.6 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 7East rim: the great root and the curtain behind. The root that sticks out into the well is the end of a root as thick as a man's body that runs back along the top of the far door. Behind the three roots you catch hang three more, each as thick as a thigh, too thick to take for a rope, with moss on them. Together they are the curtain: you fly into its front edge. (1.3 to 4.0 m behind you)
  • 8East rim: the garden through the door. Past the roots: glowing moss on the rock, a spore lantern's green light, and the first spotted mushroom, a small one, orange with pale spots. At the foot of the curtain one last small cluster of crystal stands dull and grey with roots grown round it. Green light falls on the east wall of the well and stops at its edge, as the violet does on the west. (1.5 to 3.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 9The horn. Rough pale lilac from end to end, six-sided, 0.9 m thick, with growth rings across it every few metres and a clean point. It is the palest and longest thing in the cave, and the only crystal that grew sideways along your path and not out of the wall at you.
  • 10The well's two walls. Black, wet and glossy from top to bottom on both sides, with white drips. Nothing on them can be held. The frosted sill stops dead at the well's edge, and the black starts.
  • 11The three roots. Tan, hairy and dry, each as thick as a wrist: the garden's own picture of something you can hold. They hang 12 m from the root above. The dashed ring is where the fling from the horn's tip arrives.
  • 12The root sill. A mat of roots and moss 2 m thick that fills the foot of the far door and stands 1 m out into the well. Dry and tan on top. It is where you step off the roots.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 13West: one dark crystal point. Standing in front of the well's west wall below the sill, in front of solid rock. Its tip stops 2.7 m under the sill. It is the cave's last piece of foreground. (1.3 to 2.2 m in front)
  • 14East: a clump of fern. At the bottom right corner, in front of the garden floor's own rock, with its tips below the floor's top. It is the garden's first piece of foreground. One side of the frame has crystal in front and the other has green. (1.3 to 2.0 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • Mist turning slowly in the well below the sill, lit violet on the west side and green on the east.
  • White drips falling down both walls of the well, with no sound of landing.
  • A few green spores drifting west across the shaft: the first green thing you see is one landing on the horn.
  • The roots sway a little by themselves, and more when one is caught.
