La Gruta de Cristal · Area and background design

Rest 3.0: La Geoda de la Rana

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The frog's room. It is a geode: a hollow ball of stone, 3 m across inside, lined with crystal points, that sat whole inside the cave's west wall. At some time the face of the wall broke away here and took a slice off the geode's side. The slice lies on the floor behind. What is left in the wall is a round room with one side open, and the frog lives in it. The room is on the short piece of floor west of the hole you come up through: the side of the hole nobody has a reason to go to. You hear the frog croak as you come off the rungs.

What must not change

  • The hole, the rungs up through it, and the two pieces of floor either side of it stay exactly as they are.
  • The wall above the room stays glass that can't be held.
  • The room adds one thing: a level floor 2.3 m long inside the wall, at the height of the floor outside, behind a way in 2.2 m tall. It is not on the way to anywhere.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The inside of the geode. A round hollow in the wall rock, 2.9 m across and 2.8 m tall, cut away at the front like every room you can walk into: floor, back and roof, no front wall. Its back is the far half of the same ball. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 2The lining. Short crystal points all round the inside, every one pointing at the middle. They are clear and deep violet, with a faint light of their own. The room is the only place in the cave where crystal is on every side of you. (1.2 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 3The frog. It sits on a stump of crystal at the back of the room and looks at itself in one clear face, 0.9 m tall (note 60). It breathes, blinks, turns to watch you, and croaks. (1.6 to 2.2 m behind you)
  • 4The room's light. One bright white point hanging from the top of the hollow. There is no lantern: the frog's lamp is a crystal, like every light in the cave. (1.6 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 5The rind. The geode's shell shows on the wall's face as rings of banded stone round the way in, dark blue-grey, the way a cut geode shows its rings. It tells you from across the hole that this is a geode and not a cave mouth. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 6The slice that broke off. The side of the geode, lying hollow side up on the back edge of the short floor like a dish 2.4 m across, lined with the same points. It is why the room is open. It replaces the cluster that stands here today. (1.4 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 7A mirror face over the hole. A clear face 4 m wide on the hall's back wall, straight behind the top of the rungs. The first thing the cave shows you is yourself, small, climbing out of the floor. The game has this face today. (4.3 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 8A giant, far back. One of the cave's far crystals, bigger than a house, dim and blue, standing on the hall floor well behind everything. It is there today. (5.0 to 8.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 9The room's floor. New. Crystal has filled the bottom of the geode level, and its top is frosted and pale like the floor outside, so it reads as somewhere to stand. It is the only new thing to stand on in the whole biome, and only the room needs it.
  • 10The way in. Where the wall's face cuts the geode: an opening 2.2 m tall, so you walk in upright. Its edge is the broken rind, rough and pale for a hand's width, then the wall's dark glass above.
  • 11The kerb by the hole. Today a small white box on the east edge of the hole. It becomes a short rough point of pale crystal, the same size, standing up out of the floor.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
