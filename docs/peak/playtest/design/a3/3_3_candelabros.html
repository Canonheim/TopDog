La Gruta de Cristal · Area and background design

Challenge 3.3: Candelabros

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The top of the start hall, under its sloping roof. The floor you walked in on is 23 to 30 m straight down. This is the first place in the cave where somebody has been before you. The people who built the temple higher up came down here for crystal, and they hung chandeliers under this roof to light the hall while they worked. Three are left on the line you cross. Each is a ring of bright crystal points on a bronze chain, and each chain hangs from a carved stone pug let into the roof. They still give light. Everything else in sight grew here.

What must not change

  • Three chandeliers, 6 m apart, each hung 3.2 m higher than the last, on chains 8.7 m long from the roof.
  • The chains and the chandeliers can be held. The roof and the knobs the chains hang from can't.
  • The rest at each end: the crystal above the mirror, and the one before the geode with its frosted west face.
  • The glass crystal hanging from the roof east of the last chandelier, which stops you flying over the geode.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The lamp line, going back. The same carved bosses carry on along the roof behind the three you use. Two still hold their chandeliers, hauled up tight under the roof so no chain shows, glowing white. One is an empty bronze ring. Nothing thin hangs behind the climb: a far chain would read as one more thing to catch. (3.5 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 2A mirror face that doubles the lights. A clear face 5 m wide on the back wall, below the second and third chandeliers. When a chandelier swings, its light swings in the face, and so do you. (4.3 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 3An offering niche at the top rest. A carved hollow 0.6 m tall in the back wall at head height, with a small crystal set in it and a ring of jade round the crystal. It is the first temple work you see close up. One stands beside every piece of temple work in the cave. (2.6 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 4The roots of the two rests. Each rest is the end of a fat pale crystal, with its fan of smaller points on a rock collar where it leaves the wall, like the steps of the stair. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 5The points of two giants, below. The top of the mirror crystal, and one more further east. You are level with their points now. They are dim, blue and turned away from you. (4.0 to 8.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 6The three bosses. Today each chain hangs from a glass knob under the roof. Each knob becomes the temple's boss: a carved stone pug 0.9 m across holding a bronze ring in its mouth. Here the cave has grown a skin of clear crystal over the stone, so it is glossy and dark, and still reads as something you can't hold.
  • 7The chandeliers and their chains. As the game has them: a ring of crystal points on a bronze hoop, 1.2 m across, hung on bronze chain. The points are bright white, the brightest things in the hall. One small ring of jade sits where each chain meets its hoop. Chain in this cave always means you can hold it.
  • 8The far rest's frosted face. The west face of the rest you land on is rough and pale under its lip, so a low catch still holds. It is the same rough lilac as every hold.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • Each chandelier's light moves with it: the shadows of the roof crystals swing when it swings.
  • A soft ring of glass notes when a chandelier is caught, like a wind chime.
  • The start floor far below stays lit, so the drop can be read.
