La Mina Vieja · Area and background design

Rest 1.0b: The landing stage and the incline

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The east end of the Sump, and the way back up. When the bottom level was dry, ore was hauled from it to the main gallery up a steep track, by a hand winch at the top. The rails are gone, but the sleepers are still spiked to the slope, and they are your steps. At the foot is the landing stage, the one dry plank at the water's edge, where the miners tied up the flat boat they used once the water had come. To reach it you swim under El Diente, through what was the bottom level's tunnel through the foot of the pillar. Everyone who falls into the Sump comes out here. The waterfall is 50 m behind you by now: a noise, and a cold light on the water.

What must not change

  • The incline, its six sleepers and its steepness. The landing stage and the last pile.
  • The swim under the pillar, with 2.5 m of air over the water.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The cavern's wall. The same hand-cut wall as behind Los Tablones, 20 to 34 m below the walk. Down here it is black and wet, and the lower walk's row of empty sockets is far overhead. (2.9 to 3.6 m behind you)
  • 2The tunnel through the pillar's foot. The space you swim through under El Diente was cut by the miners: the bottom level's road, 11 m long, with four timber sets. Their posts stand in the water and their caps are just under the rock. So the pillar stands on a timbered tunnel, not on a gap. (1.3 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 3The back rail, and a cart left on it. Behind the sleepers one rail of the old track is still spiked down, from the water to the gallery's mouth, and a grey ore cart sits on it a third of the way up, where the winch let it stop. (1.4 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 4The winch at the head of the incline. In its cut beside the gallery's mouth, as on the camp's page. Its drum is bare. (1.3 to 2.9 m behind you)
  • 5The drowned level. The bottom level's timber sets, carried on east of the pillar along the wall, under water to their caps. (2.0 to 2.9 m behind you)
  • 6A lamp post and the pug arrow. At the back of the landing stage. The lantern that hangs in the air here today gets a post to hang from. Under it the mine's arrow sign points up the incline. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 7The miners' boat. A flat boat 3 m long, sunk at its mooring behind the landing stage, with one end still out of the water. (1.4 to 2.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8Spike plates and rust lines. Each sleeper gets two iron plates where the rails were spiked, and the slope between gets two faint rust lines. The slope reads as a track with its rails taken up.
  • 9The landing stage. A plank deck on two short piles, with an iron mooring ring at its end.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 10The near bank. The same low lip of black wet rock as at the Sump's west end, along the bottom of the frame and below the pool's floor. (1.3 to 3.2 m in front)
