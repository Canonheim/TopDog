La Mina Vieja · Area and background design

Challenge 1.8: Las Vigas

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Under the flat roof of the cavern, 78 m above the water, the miners hung one long beam on four posts bolted into the roof. It ran from above the track's end to the landing under the winding wheel, and the deck of the track's long crossing hung from it on chains. When the deck fell, its weight snapped the beam in three places. The four pieces are still there, each on its own post, each sagging to a different height, with 2.5 m of air between one splintered end and the next. A second beam at the back never broke, and shows the line the first one had. You go along what is left the only way there is: swing under a piece, let go, land on the next. The waterfall is in front of you the whole way.

What must not change

  • The four beams: where each hangs, how long it is, how high.
  • The one chain on the first beam (Parker, 11-13), and the staging you land on at the end.
  • Nothing between you and the water.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The waterfall. Straight ahead, 6 m past the last beam, and you fling towards it three times. From here it is a sheet 6 m wide, white, with the winding wheel turning slowly in its spray at the top left. It lights the beams from in front. (1.2 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 2The back beam. The twin of the beam you are crossing, close under the roof and all in one piece, 18.5 m long. It shows what the four pieces were. Along it are the empty iron eyes the other chains hung from. No chain hangs from any of them. (1.5 to 1.9 m behind you)
  • 3Four hanger posts. One behind the middle of each piece, from the piece up to the roof. Today a post in hold-coloured timber stands in the middle of the play space on top of each beam. It moves to the back and goes grey, so the top of a beam is clear to land on. (1.2 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 4Two lanterns. On iron brackets on the hanger posts of the first and third pieces, at the back. Today they hang inside the play space, just over the beams. (1.3 to 1.5 m behind you)
  • 5Stalactites, kept off the route. The roof's stalactites hang only east of the first beam. By the course's numbers a 6 m one is placed at the roof between the second and third beams today. That one goes: nothing may hang in the air you fling through. (1.5 to 1.9 m behind you)

Play space

  • 6One chain. Parker, 11-13: one chain hangs from the first beam, not two. It is the one you arrive on from the cart: a real chain with links, rusty red, 9 m long. The near chain (crossed out here) is taken away.
  • 7Splintered ends. Every end that faces a gap is splintered. The two outer ends are sawn square. So the four read as one beam, broken in three places.
  • 8Iron stirrups. A rust-orange iron stirrup round each piece where its hanger post takes it. It is what the piece hangs in, and it is the one spot on each beam that looks strong.
  • 9Bolt plates in the roof. Drawn on the roof above each post: an iron plate with four bolt heads.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
