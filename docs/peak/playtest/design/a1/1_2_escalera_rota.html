La Mina Vieja · Area and background design

Challenge 1.2: Escalera Rota

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The men's way up from the gallery to the workings above: a ladder shaft 4 m wide and 13 m tall, lined with planks. It was always the wet shaft, because the water that gets into the mine runs down its east side, so the ladder in it is iron where every other ladder in the mine is wood. It was built in four lengths, with a timber landing between each length and the next. The iron lasted. The landings did not. Each one rotted, fell, and tore out the ladder's joint as it went, and those are the three gaps you lunge across.

What must not change

  • Every rung where it is, and the three gaps: 1.5 m, 1.88 m and 2.05 m.
  • The slick east wall, and the ledge at the head of the ladder.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The shaft's back wall. Plank lining on a timber frame every 1.3 m, the same as every shaft in the mine. Here it is whole, but black with wet and green at the seams, with two planks gone. (2.4 to 2.7 m behind you)
  • 2Three rotted landings. Behind each gap in the ladder: what is left of a timber landing, its east half still in place and its west half hanging down. Each length of ladder went from one landing to the next. The three gaps are the three landings. On each one lies the bent iron rung that came out of the ladder there. (1.3 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 3A lantern on the top landing. Today it hangs in the middle of the shaft, in the play space, from nothing. It stands on what is left of the top landing, so the longest gap is the best lit. (1.6 to 1.9 m behind you)
  • 4The frame at the head of the shaft. Two posts and a cap at the back, 2.6 m tall, round the top of the shaft. You see it from the foot of the ladder, and it marks where the climb ends. The pickaxe that lies in the play space on the ledge today leans against its west post. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 5A pug arrow sign. On the ledge at the head of the ladder, pointing west along the catwalk. The kit has it. (1.3 to 1.4 m behind you)
  • 6The cavern. West of the rock the shaft is cut in, the cavern opens: 20 m of air down to the Sump's beach, and the far wall a long way back, cold grey-violet. At the head of the ladder you step out into it. The waterfall is still out of sight behind the pillar, but its light is on everything to the west. (6.0 to 6.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7Torn rail ends. At each gap the ladder's two iron rails end in torn, rusted points, above and below. The ladder is plainly one ladder with three pieces missing. Today a bent rung hangs in each gap, in the play space, and holds nothing. Those three go from the gaps and lie on the landings behind.
  • 8Empty sockets. In the rock beside the two upper gaps: the square socket the landing's beam sat in, empty. From below you can read where the gaps are by the sockets.
  • 9The wet east wall. The wall you can't hold: glossy black with pale drips, because the water that comes down the slope at the top runs down this side. Nothing pale is drawn on it.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 10The shaft's two front posts. Thin, each in three broken lengths, in front of the rock at the two edges of the shaft and never in front of the shaft itself. The same piece as El Derrumbe's. (1.2 to 1.5 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • Water running down the east wall, and a slow drip off each hanging landing.
  • Rust flakes falling from a rail end when you take the rung under it.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • No note from the playtest was about this challenge. The ladder was remade before it, with real rails and missing rungs.
  • In the play space today and moved out of it: the lantern in mid shaft (to stand on the top landing), the pickaxe on the ledge (to lean on the frame's post), and the three bent rungs that hang in the gaps (to lie on the landings behind). A bent rung can't become a real hold: it would halve the gap it hangs in.
  • The ladder's two rails stay drawn and not held, which is how the ladder was asked for before the playtest. Only the rungs hold.
  • Nothing you touch moves.
