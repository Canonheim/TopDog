La Mina Vieja · Area and background design

Challenge 1.6: Vetas de Lodo

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The east face of El Diente, the pillar of rock the miners left standing to hold up the roof. The gold in this mine lies in thin slanting seams of hard pale rock with soft wet clay between them, and eight of those seams cross the pillar. On this face the clay has washed out as black mud, and the hard seams stand proud of it as ribs, 0.6 to 1.2 m out. You climb the ends of the seams. The miners dug along the very same seams in the cavern's wall, and their workings are there behind you, at the same heights.

What must not change

  • The eight ribs, each at its height and its length, and the black face between them.
  • The two long lunges to the two wide ribs.
  • The fall from here: through the gap beside the station deck, to the pillar's shoulder 20 m down.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The cavern's wall. The same wall as behind the lift, cold grey-violet. The same eight seams cross it, climbing to the east at the same slant as on the pillar. They are faint lines, greyer than the rock, so that none of them reads as a ledge. (2.9 to 3.6 m behind you)
  • 2Two old workings. Where the miners followed the two richest seams into the wall: each seam is painted as a darker band 1.1 m wide on the wall, with short timber props standing in front of it. Painted, not cut in (the walls rule: no dark recess in a face). They are level with the two wide ribs, so each long lunge has a band behind its target. (2.9 to 4.6 m behind you)
  • 3A lantern in the lower working. It lights the face from the side, so every rib throws a shadow under itself. Today a lantern hangs inside the play space about a metre from the ribs, from nothing, where it could be taken for a hold. That one goes. (3.0 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 4The station below. El Cubo's station: the dark frame of the hoistway, and the crates on the back of the deck you start from. (1.3 to 2.9 m behind you)

Play space

  • 5The seams across the pillar. Drawn on the pillar's blank face: each rib's seam, a pale slanting band 0.4 m thick, running back across the whole pillar from the rib's root. You read the ribs as the ends of eight bands, not as eight shelves stuck on a wall.
  • 6The two wide ribs. The two you lunge for get a row of old drill holes along their edge. The miners started on these two and stopped. All eight ribs are pale, chalky and dry, with a broken edge.
  • 7The mud between. Black, glossy clay with pale drips, oozing from under each rib. Nothing pale is drawn on it between one rib and the next.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.
