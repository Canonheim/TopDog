La Mina Vieja · Area and background design

Challenge 1.1: La Galería

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

This was the mine's main road: the gallery the ore carts ran along to the tunnel and out to the station. It is 5 m tall and timbered in two storeys, with a set of posts every 1.3 m because the miners knew this stretch was bad ground. They were right. Mud burst through the back wall, took 10 m of the floor down with it, and the lower posts of four sets went down too. What you cross on is what stayed up: the cross pieces of the sets either side of the hole, hanging from their upper posts, and the heads of the four sunk posts standing in the mud. It is the first place the mine asks you to tell honey-coloured timber from black mud.

What must not change

  • The five low beams and the four posts in the mud, each exactly where it is.
  • The pit: its two slick slopes, its depth, and the pale floor at its bottom that the two middle posts climb out of.
  • The floor either side, and the door from the cart tunnel.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The gallery's back wall. Plank lining from floor to roof, held by the timber sets. A long tie timber runs along it at head height, at exactly the height of the beams you hold: they are one row. Over the pit the lining has burst outward and the tie is gone. That black hole is where the mud came from. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 2The back posts. Behind every beam stands its back post, with an iron strap where the beam is let into it. Over the pit the lower halves of the posts are gone and the beams hang from the upper halves. Where four sets went down altogether, two stubs are left under the roof. Today a post in hold-coloured timber hangs in the middle of the play space above each beam. It moves back to the wall and goes grey. (1.2 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 3The slump and the cart. The pit carries on back behind you as a tongue of black mud coming down out of the burst wall. In it, half sunk: an ore cart on its side, and the bent ends of the rails, which stop at the brink on both sides. The cart is in the play space today, in iron's own colour. It moves back and goes grey. (1.3 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 4The ore chute. High in the back wall by the door: a dark hole, and a timber chute coming down out of it on two wall brackets, to fill the carts that stood on the rails below. It says what the rails are for, and why there is a cart in the mud. (1.3 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 5A warning sign. The mine's skull-pug diamond, nailed to the lining at the brink of the pit. It is the first one you meet. The kit has it. (1.3 to 1.4 m behind you)
  • 6Two lanterns. Where they hang today, one over each brink, but hung from the roof timber at the back wall and not in the play space. Between them the pit is the darkest part of the gallery. (1.3 to 1.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7The door's timber set (there today). The last set of the cart tunnel and the first of the mine: a back post, a front post and a cap, and you walk between the posts. It stays. It is grey and wet, as the tunnel's sets have become by here.
  • 8The four sunk posts. Each post standing in the mud keeps the cut tongue on its head where a beam once sat, and is black with mud up to a tide line. You can see they are the lower posts of the four missing sets, gone down 2 m with the floor.
  • 9The beams' torn ends. Each of the five beams shows its end to the camera, splintered where its front post tore away. The front posts are in the pit.
  • 10The mud. Black, glossy and wet from brink to bottom, with slide marks and pale drips, and nothing pale drawn anywhere on it, so the pit reads as a pit (note 52). The floor at the bottom is a slab of the old gallery floor, dry and pale, with a sleeper still on it.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 11A low lip of rubble. Mud-caked rubble and a length of bent rail along the bottom of the frame, below the floor and below the pit. It stands in front of rock only. (1.3 to 2.0 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • A slow bubble now and then in the mud at the back of the pit.
  • One drip from the roof onto the pit's floor.
  • Cold blue light and a little mist at the far west end of the gallery, where it opens on the cavern.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 52: this pit already can't be climbed out of by its sides. They are slick mud. The design keeps every pale mark off them.
