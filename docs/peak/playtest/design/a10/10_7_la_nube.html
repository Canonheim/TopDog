La Cresta de la Tormenta · Area and background design

Challenge 10.7: La Nube

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

One cloud never leaves the buttress. It sits on the buttress's east shoulder, between the last tower and the balloon, and the way up goes through the middle of it. The cloud is behind you, close: a wall of painted grey 1.5 m back, with the far view gone. Out of it stands a rib of dark rock, and up the rib runs a vein of white quartz that has left five knobs, each one a step east and a step up from the last. The rib is cracked across two thirds of the way up, and the rock above the crack has slipped: so the fourth knob is not where the first three say it will be. It is higher, and back to the west. You can see all of this. Nothing here is hidden, and the only test is whether you look before you reach. From the fifth knob the balloon's rope hangs within reach, and it goes up out of the top of the cloud into gold light.

What must not change

  • The five knobs, each exactly where it is. The fourth stays out of line.
  • The buttress's scar and foothold, where you start, and the reach from the fifth knob to the rope's foot.
  • The wind from 943.2 to 953 m, which blows east here, away from the buttress.
  • The cloud stays behind the climb. Nothing hides you or a hold.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The cloud. One painted bank, 24 m wide and 11 m tall, 1.5 m back. It fills the frame from side to side and from the bottom up to 951 m. It is painted as cloud: big soft billows in three rows, darkest at the foot, in mid greys and slate blues, with nothing near white, and no straight edge anywhere. It does not move across you. The two dark cards that stand here today are replaced by it. (1.5 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 2The buttress's shoulder, seen through the cloud. Painted on the bank, one tone darker than the cloud: the shape of the buttress's body, rising behind the rib. The rib is the near edge of it. It has no outline and no detail. It is rock seen through cloud, and it says that the knobs are on a mountain and not on a card. (1.5 to 1.5 m behind you)
  • 3The summit's teeth, standing out of the cloud. Above the cloud's top, the lowest ends of the rock teeth of the summit's crown, 1.4 m back. They are the summit's pieces and are designed on its pages. Today each one stops square at 958 m, in the air, and the summit's pages carry them down. This design asks that they stop at 953 m, behind the cloud's top, so that the crown stands out of the storm and nothing of it comes down across this climb. (1.4 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 4The cloud's top. The top 4 m of the cloud is its own strip: a row of round heads, their tops between 953 and 954.6 m, standing 1.3 m back, a hand in front of the bank. Above it the rain stops and the light is gold. It is a separate piece so that it can stand in front of the feet of the summit's teeth, which are 1.4 m back. (1.3 to 1.4 m behind you)
  • 5The rib. A fin of dark wet slate just behind the play space, 2 m wide, rising from the foothold to the top of the cloud. It is the darkest thing in the picture, and the white knobs stand on it. Painted on it in white: the quartz vein, a straight line through the first three knobs. Then a clean black crack across the rib. Then the vein again, moved 1.3 m to the west and 0.6 m up, through the last two knobs. The break in the vein points at the fourth knob. The rib's east edge is cut back 0.4 m near its top, so no rock stands directly behind the balloon's rope. (1.2 to 1.3 m behind you)
  • 6A wind sock at the foothold. Note 89. This wind's flag where you start: a grey canvas sock on a short stub at the back of the foothold. It streams east, away from the buttress, 1.3 m below the first knob. (1.2 to 1.3 m behind you)
  • 7The winch. At the top of the frame, on the buttress's top step: the balloon's winch. It is on El Globo's page. (1.3 to 2.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The five knobs. Knots of white quartz, 0.56 m across and 0.3 m proud, dry and matte. They are the palest things in the picture. On the dark rib each one shows from the foothold.
  • 9The scar and the foothold. Where the third flight of Gendarmes lands: 1.5 m of pale dry slate on the buttress's east side, over a foothold 1 m wide. It is the only perch before the cloud. The buttress above and below it is blue-black and glossy.
  • 10The rope's foot. The end of the balloon's one rope, with a small sandbag on it. It comes down out of the cloud's top and swings between 949 and 951 m as the balloon bobs. In a gust it leans east, away from the fifth knob, so it is the wind's flag at the last reach. The rope is on El Globo's page.
  • 11The wind, drawn. Note 89. Long wavy white lines with an inked edge, moving east, from the buttress's side to the edge of the screen. They show plainly against the cloud because of their inked edge. The headwall's wind shows above 953 m, going east as well.

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • Rain falls straight through the picture up to 952 m and leans east in a gust.
  • The cloud goes pale for an instant when lightning takes the mast, far to the east and below. The rib and the knobs do not change.
  • Sound is close and dull inside the cloud. The wind's whistle is the clearest thing in it.
  • Thin wisps drift along the face of the bank, behind you. None of them ever crosses in front of you.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 92: cloud is painted as cloud and stands behind the climb. Note 40: nothing stands in front of where a climber can be.
  • The blind version of this climb, with the scene dimmed and a flash every 4 seconds, was taken out on 11-12 and its cloud moved behind the climb. This design keeps that. The line for 10.7 in the list of challenges still describes the blind version.
  • Note 89: the wind is drawn across the climb, and it has a flag at each end: a sock at the foothold and the rope's sandbag at the last reach.
  • The fourth knob is the whole challenge. With nothing hidden it could pass unnoticed, so the rib says it out loud: a crack, and the vein stepping over.
  • Course changes, scenery only: one painted bank in place of the two dark cards. The rib's east edge is cut back 0.4 m near its top. The top 4 m of the cloud stands 1.3 m back.
  • Nothing you touch moves.
