Rest 11.6b: The ending: the ceremony of the belt

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The top of the bell frame's post, at the moment your hand touches the belt. The climb is over and nothing more is asked of you. A ceremony plays on your climber. The belt comes off its pegs into your hands, and you lift it over your head with both arms. Holding it up, you turn slowly on the spot and rise off the post, 6 m into the air above the summit. Long flat rays fan out behind you and wheel round as you turn, in cream, yellow and the colours of the town's paper. An orchestra plays the mountain's four-note call, resolved at last, with the summit's mariachi and the bell in it, and the bell tolls three times on its own. Far below in the east the town's fireworks come up through the cloud. Then the TOP DOG card with your time and your falls, the confetti, and the wait for a button, as today. And then the game goes on: the button lifts the card and sets you down on the post's top with the clock stopped. West of you the steam stands up out of the notch, and the frogs' spring is a step off the end of the beam.

What must not change

  • Touching the belt ends the climb, exactly as today. The ceremony asks for no button and no hold.
  • After it: the TOP DOG card with your time and your falls, the confetti, the wait for a button, and your flag on the Peak behind the title screen.
  • Then the button sets you down on the post's top, with your time kept and the clock stopped, free to go west to the frogs' spring (note 102).
  • The three tolls of the bell at the finish.
  • No glowing material anywhere. The rays are flat colour.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The rays. Eight flat cut-out blades, each 12 to 30 m long and 1 to 3 m wide at its far end, fanned out from your climber's chest and standing 1.5 m behind you. They are cream, yellow, and five of the town's paper colours, with the kit's inked cut edge, bright against the sky. They open one after another as you leave the post, and they wheel round as you turn, the same way and at the same speed. They pass behind the frame, the bell and the dome, and in front of the paper and the staff. (1.5 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 2The peak's own back. The head of the mountain behind the frame, and its west flank rising away at the left edge. In the wide frame of the ceremony it is small. The sky and the far view fill the rest. (3.0 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 3The bell staff and the two lines of paper. The staff's top is 1.2 m under your feet when you have risen. The two lines of paper fall away from it to the east and to the west, so you hang over the place where they meet. Every paper lifts and flutters while the orchestra plays. (1.3 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 4The town's fireworks. Far away in the east and far below, small cut-out bursts come up through the sea of cloud, a few at a time, in the town's colours. The town is under the cloud and can't be seen from here. This is how you know that it saw you. They are the far town's own fireworks, and they stand far behind everything of ours. (7.0 to 8.0 m behind you)

Play space

  • 5Your climber, turning, with the belt held up. Your climber is the game's climber model, posed: nothing bespoke is modelled, and the new climber model takes its place when it comes. The belt is held by a hand on each strap end, its plates toward the camera, lifted right over the head. The climber turns slowly on the spot, to the left, once in about 4 seconds, and rises 6 m in a straight line from the post's top. At the top of the rise the climber stays there, still turning. The dashed line is the rise.
  • 6The empty pegs. The belt is one thing: when it comes into your hands it is gone from the post, and the two bronze pegs stand bare.
  • 7The bell, tolling on its own. The bell swings itself for the three tolls, clapper showing, with nobody on the rope. The rope sways under it.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 8The TOP DOG card and the confetti. After the ceremony the card drops in front of everything, with your time and your falls, and the confetti falls, as today. Behind the card your climber is still in the air, turning, with the rays wheeling. Nothing stands in front of the climber until then. (3.0 to 3.2 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • The camera pulls back as you rise, to the width it has for the last leap, and centres 4 m above the post, so the whole fan of rays is on screen.
  • Marigold petals lift off the frame and go up with you.
  • Gold dust thickens round the climber. It is dust, not a glow.
  • Every line of paper on the summit lifts and flutters while the orchestra plays.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 101: the ending is a ceremony played on the climber. Today nothing happens to the climber: the bell tolls three times with a short fanfare, the card drops in and the belt stays on the post.
  • A first try at the order, about 10 seconds in all, which is the length the note's plan gives. At once: the first toll, the orchestra starts, and the belt comes off its pegs into both hands. By 1.5 seconds: the belt is over your head. From 1.5 to 9 seconds: you turn and rise 6 m, the rays open and wheel, the second and third tolls come, and the fireworks begin. At 10 seconds: the card and the confetti, with the climber still turning behind them. How long it runs and whether a button skips it are settled when it is built, as the sheet says.
  • Note 100: the three tolls at the belt stay as the finish. In the design the bell is seen to make them.
  • The area's own sun and its pale sky card stay out. The rays are seen against the far view's sky.
  • Parker: a new climber model is coming very soon. The ceremony is the game's climber model, posed.
  • Note 102 and the order of things. Touching the belt ends the run with the TOP DOG card, so for the frogs' spring to be the reward past the belt, the game goes on after the card: the button puts you back on the post and you can walk west. Your time is the time to the belt. The other way, taking the way west before the belt, would put the reward before the ending.
  • Nothing you touch moves. The box round the belt that ends the climb is as it is today.
