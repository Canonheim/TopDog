La Cumbre · Area and background design

Challenge 11.6: Top Dog

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The last 4 m of the mountain. From the bell you pull up onto the frame's beam, walk it west to the post, and step up onto the post's top, which stands 0.7 m above the beam. The belt is there, held up on two bronze pegs, with its plates toward you: gold on red leather, made in the chimney's forge 500 m below and carried up here by the people who built the ring. It has waited on this post ever since. Nothing here is hard. It is three reaches on dry timber, with the whole climb under you and nothing above you but a staff and the sky. Touch the belt and the climb is over.

What must not change

  • The three reaches: from the bell onto the beam, along the beam, and up onto the post's top.
  • The belt where it is, and the box round it that ends the climb when you touch it.
  • The beam and the post are held all over.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The head of the peak's back. The mountain's own rock, 3 m behind, coming to a blunt head at 1,000.8 m behind the post's foot. It is the top of the mountain. Everything above it was carried up. (3.0 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 2The frame's back strut. The timber that ties the post back to the rock, as on La Campana's page. (1.3 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 3The bell staff. Behind the post, raked back, rising 4.8 m above its top. It is thin and honey-coloured, and it stands 2 m behind the belt, so the belt is seen against the sky and not against the staff. (1.3 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 4The two lines of paper. They leave the staff's top to the east and to the west, falling away on both sides, so that the last thing you climb toward is the place where all the town's paper meets. Nothing is tied to the beam or the post. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)

Play space

  • 5The belt on its pegs. Gold plates on red leather, 1.2 m wide and 0.6 m tall, with the pug's face on its centre plate. It stands upright on two bronze pegs 0.5 m tall in the post's top, its plates toward you. It is the forge's work, and nothing else up here looks like it. Brushed gold, matte, like the crown's bosses: the last hold on the mountain.
  • 6The beam's top. Honey timber, 0.4 m deep, squared with an adze, with the garland pressed flat along its front face. You come onto it at its east end, beside the yoke, and walk 4 m to the post. West of the post it runs on 1.1 m: after the card, that end is where you step off for the frogs' spring, under the frog's painted sign.
  • 7The post's head. The post stands 0.7 m above the beam, and its top is 0.5 m square: room for two feet. This is the last step.
  • 8The bell you step off. The bell and its yoke, as on La Campana's page. You come up the bell's flank and the yoke onto the beam's east end.
  • 9The box that ends the climb. A hand anywhere in this box, 1.5 m square round the belt, ends the climb, as today. The ceremony starts here. It is on the next page.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 10The tip of the summit's snow cap (there today). The top 1.3 m of the white cap, in front of the ice of the very top, under the post's foot. It covers nothing you touch. (2.0 to 2.0 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • The bell behind you is still rocking, and tolls softly once as you step off it.
  • Gold dust. The paper overhead lifts toward the east.
  • The belt's plates catch the low sun as you come level with them. That is colour on the plates, not a glow.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 101: touching the belt starts the ceremony. It is designed on the next page. What you do to reach the belt does not change.
  • Note 102: the way west to the frogs' spring is past the belt. You see its steam from the beam, and its sign hangs on the frame's back strut.
  • The garland along the beam hangs 0.4 m in front of the timber today, in the play space, 0.5 m thick. It is pressed flat onto the beam's front face.
  • The belt's model floats 0.5 m above the post's top today. It stays where it is and gets two bronze pegs to stand on.
  • Nothing you touch moves.
