La Estación · Area and background design

Challenge 0.3c: El Descenso, part three: the wet tunnel and the gap

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The lower tunnel runs along the edge of the waterfall cavern, and the cavern's water is getting in. The sets are grey, the lining has planks missing, and a seep of black mud has come through the back wall. It is the first slick thing in the game, shown where you can't touch it. Then, for 8 m, the back wall is gone. It fell out into the cavern and took one timber set with it. Through the hole you get one look at where you are going: the far wall of a very big room, the long white line of the waterfall, and a few lantern specks on the workings beside it. Then the wall closes again.

What must not change

  • The track's line: down at 11 degrees to x -120, then at 6 degrees to x -136.
  • The cart's speed and its path.
  • The lantern lights at x -120 and -136.
  • The bed grips: it stays dry, pale and matte however wet the wall behind it is.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The tunnel's back wall, wet. Grey plank lining with planks missing and wet rock behind. The trough along its foot carries the water on down. Drips fall from the caps. (1.7 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 2The gap. 8 m of the back wall broken out, with a ragged edge and a low sill of rubble. One whole set went with it, so the roof is bare there. The back posts of two other sets still stand in the opening, dark against the view. At the cart's speed the gap is on screen for about 3 seconds. (1.7 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 3The waterfall cavern. Seen through the gap, 14 m back and more: the cold grey-violet far wall, the waterfall as one pale line from above the frame to below it, mist at its foot, and five lantern specks on the workings beside it. It is a far cut-out, like the Peak at the station. It is the only cool light on the ride. (14.0 to 40.0 m behind you)
  • 4The black streak. A seep of glossy black mud down the back wall between two sets, 2 m wide at the floor, with pale drips on it. The lanterns either side light it. It is on the back wall only, never on the bed. (1.7 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 5A boarded side tunnel. The mine's way of closing a passage: two planks nailed across a dark opening. The mine's kit has it. (1.7 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 6The timber sets, grey. Grey and wet, some leaning a little. One cap just below the gap is cracked and held up by an extra prop. From here on these are the mine's sets. (1.3 to 1.7 m behind you)
  • 7Two lanterns. Amber now, and dim. The gap sits half way between them, so their light does not fight the view. The second is the tunnel's seventh and last. (1.3 to 1.5 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The dry bed. With all this wet behind it, the bed and the rails are drawn dry: pale rock, rust-orange rails, dry sleepers. Nothing dark or glossy is put on the floor here, because the floor grips.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 9Two front posts and a stump. The front post of every fourth set, so one every 10.4 m. Each is 0.3 m wide and plain. At the cart's speed one sweeps past about every 1.2 seconds, and there are never more than two on screen. The back post of the same set stands behind it, so you pass between the two. All the other sets have a back post and a cap only. Here: two, in grey wood, and the one that would stand in front of the gap is a 0.5 m stump. It went when the wall did. So from x -119 to x -140 nothing stands in front of you: the longest clear stretch of the ride, placed where the look is. (1.3 to 1.6 m in front)
