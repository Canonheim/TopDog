La Estación · Area and background design

Challenge 0.3a: El Descenso, part one: the dock, the tunnel mouth and the first drop

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The ride starts in the open. The cart runs 27 m west along the dock, in the sun, with the Peak's foot coming up to meet it, and goes into the mountain through the tunnel mouth. The mouth is the mine's first timber set, the same two posts and a cap as every set inside, whitewashed here because this end belongs to the town. Inside, the tunnel tips downhill at once. For the first 26 m the sets and the plank lining are still white, the first lantern is bright, and one last string of bunting follows you in and stops. This was the haulage way. Full carts were hauled up it by mule to the dock, and the empties ran down by their own weight, as yours does.

What must not change

  • The track's line: level along the dock to x -34, then down at 7 degrees to x -46, then down at 15 degrees to x -62.
  • The cart's speed: it is at its full 9 m a second by the time it reaches the mouth.
  • The mouth at x -36, 5 m tall. The roof stays 5 m above the bed all the way down.
  • The bed grips, so you can get out and walk the tunnel.
  • The lantern lights at x -46 and x -62.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The mountain's foot. Pale dry rock coming down to the road behind the dock's last 6 m. Above the mouth it becomes the Peak's east face, which the course already has. It is what the dock runs into. (1.7 to 6.0 m behind you)
  • 2The dock's back half, the ore hopper and the mules' trough. The back posts and the back rail carry on from the station. Against the hill stands a timber hopper on legs, where carts were tipped, with a chute down behind the dock to where the wagons stood. Beside it is a water trough, for the mules that hauled the full carts up. (1.3 to 4.4 m behind you)
  • 3The pug over the mouth. A round painted board, 1.2 m across, on an iron bracket above the cap, turned to face the camera like a shop sign. A pug painted flat over the cap would face the town, and from the side you would see only its edge. (1.3 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 4A Missing poster on the mouth's post. The fifth and last poster (Parker, 11-13), on a board nailed to the mouth's back post and turned to face you, 1.2 m above the deck. It is an old copy of the fangs, sun-bleached, with its lower third torn away. It is the last face from the town before the dark. (1.3 to 1.5 m behind you)
  • 5The tunnel's back wall. Plank lining, limewashed white as far as the second lantern. The planks are nailed on level while the floor falls away under them, so the wall itself shows you how steep the track is. (1.7 to 2.0 m behind you)
  • 6A bricked-up side passage. Between two sets, 19 m in. Bricked up and whitewashed, which is town work. Further down, a closed passage is boarded, which is the mine's way. (1.7 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 7The timber sets, whitewashed. A set every 2.6 m: a back post against the lining and a cap across the roof, posts 0.3 m square. Eleven in this stretch, counting the mouth, all whitewashed, the wash a little thinner on each one. (1.3 to 1.7 m behind you)
  • 8Two lanterns. Each hangs under a set's cap at a place where the track changes slope, so a lantern always marks a bend in the grade. The first is nearly white. Each one after it is a little dimmer and more amber. (1.3 to 1.5 m behind you)
  • 9The bunting's end. From the last pole on the dock to the pug's bracket, and one string more inside, from the mouth's cap to the cap by the first lantern. There it stops. The town comes 10 m into the mountain and no further. (1.4 to 2.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 10Whitewash on the roof. The rock roof is limewashed for the first 10 m, fading out. It is paint on dry rock, so it is still pale and matte.
  • 11The sill. Where the dock's deck meets the rock: a timber sill shod with iron, worn hollow in the middle by wheels.

Foreground (sparse)

  • 12Three front posts. The front post of every fourth set, so one every 10.4 m. Each is 0.3 m wide and plain. At the cart's speed one sweeps past about every 1.2 seconds, and there are never more than two on screen. The back post of the same set stands behind it, so you pass between the two. All the other sets have a back post and a cap only. Here: the mouth's own post and two inside, all whitewashed. (1.3 to 1.6 m in front)

Small effects, no models

  • A patch of daylight on the bed just inside the mouth, which slides back off the cart as you go in.
  • The cart's wheels change their sound at the sill: boards, then rock.
  • The last flags of the bunting flick as the cart goes under them.
