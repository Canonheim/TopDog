Rest 8.0: The frog's gondola

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Pylon 1's head. When the cableway worked, this was where the greaser stood to oil the wheels the first line turns back over, and where a spare car was kept: a short siding rail runs out from the tower's top, and one gondola is parked on it against a buffer. The car is still there. Its lantern is lit, the only lit lamp on the whole cableway, and the frog sits inside on the bench with a ticket in its hand and a small suitcase beside it: the last passenger, waiting for a car that left years ago. You stand on the perch in front of the open car. It is off the route. Nothing sends you up here but the wish to stop for a moment with something under your feet.

What must not change

  • The perch: 2.4 m of iron grating at 702.0 m, with one rail post. It is the only still thing between the low station and pylon 2.
  • Challenge 8.2 starts from here.
  • A car's roof comes up to 2.5 m under the perch every six seconds and goes away west. Nothing here stops the cars.

The three layers

Background

  • 1A stopped chair on the far line. 20 m back and hazed: the dead second line's slack cable, with one chair hanging on it where it stopped. (20.0 to 20.5 m behind you)
  • 2Pylon 1's head. The top of the first tower, 2.5 m back: two legs, zigzag braces, and a pug plate at every joint. An oil can and a rag sit on the plate beside the upper guide wheel. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 3The siding. A short rail, 5 m long, hung from the tower's top between the cable and you, with a buffer on its east end. Cars were taken off the line here to be mended. (1.7 to 1.9 m behind you)
  • 4Line 1's turn. The loop's cable comes up the tower over two greyed guide wheels, 1 m across, and goes back west toward the station. The cars turn back here, 2 m behind you and under your feet. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 5The link girder and its belt. A lattice girder goes out east from the tower's top to line 2's tower, with pennants under it. A belt brings the first line's turn up to the shaft on it. It belongs to challenge 8.2. (2.3 to 2.9 m behind you)
  • 6A streamer. On the tower's top, 3 m tall, in the town's bunting colours, streaming east. (2.5 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 7The parked gondola. The same car as the ones you ride, standing still, its floor level with the perch and its open side toward you: back wall, roof, floor, a bench, and a peeling pug crest on the back wall. Its step and rack are unbolted and gone, so nothing sticks out of it that looks like a hold. (1.2 to 2.3 m behind you)
  • 8The lit lantern. It hangs from the car's roof. Amber, shown as colour on the car's walls and on the frog. Every other lantern on the cableway is cold. (1.6 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 9The frog. 0.3 m tall, sitting on the bench and looking out at the valley. It holds a paper ticket. A small suitcase with a pug sticker stands beside it, and a timetable card is pinned to the wall behind, bleached blank by the sun. (1.5 to 1.9 m behind you)

Play space

  • 10The rung. One iron rung under the perch's east end: 0.8 m long, on two straps, at 701.2 m. It is the way up from a car's roof now that the cars' hanger arms are scenery. It is new, and it is the only thing added to hold.
  • 11The perch's grating. Open iron grating, rust orange and dry, with the one rail post on its west end. Through it you see the cars come up under your boots.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • The lantern's amber on the car's walls, steady. It is the one warm light for 100 m of climb.
  • The frog blinks, and turns its head to watch each car go by.
  • The grating hums a little each time a car turns under it.
