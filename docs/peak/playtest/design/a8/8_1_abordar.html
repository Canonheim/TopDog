El Teleférico · Area and background design

Challenge 8.1: Abordar

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The low station's boarding stage. The cableway's first line is a short loop of cable between the station and pylon 1: out low, up at the pylon, back high, down at the station. It carries two gondolas and it never stops, so the town built a raised gangway on the end of the deck and put a boarding step on the end of every car: as a car came down past the gangway you took the step and it carried you off. That is still how you leave the mountain. A car comes down to the gangway every six seconds, stops for no one, and goes out east over La Garganta. The wind engine on the station's end frame is what turns the loop, and it has not stopped since the town left.

What must not change

  • A car passes the gangway every six seconds. Its step comes down to 1.0 m above the gangway's east lip and leaves east at once. It is in reach for about one second of every six.
  • The gangway: 3.3 m of planks, 0.7 m above the deck, with one rail post. A miss leaves you standing on the deck.
  • The loop and both cars exactly as the course has them: 6 m across, a lap of 12 seconds, the cars at opposite corners.
  • Pylon 1's perch, 2.4 m of iron at 702.0 m, over the loop's east end, reached as today from the top of a car's hanger.
  • Nothing new comes into reach of the deck or the gangway: the station's roof is 3.8 m over the gangway, and 0.5 m above a hand raised from the top of its rail post.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The far line's first tower. 20 m back, a flat cut-out hazed toward the sky: the first tower of the cableway's second line, rising behind the station house, with a slack cable going out east. That line is dead. Nothing else of ours stands between you and the far view. (20.0 to 20.5 m behind you)
  • 2The express lines' middle tower. It rises from behind the station's ridge and goes up out of the frame: the first sight of the lines you will ride at the top. Today it is a card 1.6 m back. It goes to 2.5 m. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 3The low station's house, east end. The cliffs' join designs the station. What matters here is where the house stands: 2.5 m back, 12 m wide, under the station's roof, with the pug sign, the cold lantern and the ticket booth on its wall. Its boards are the greyed red below bare grey boards, with no cream, because nothing of it comes within reach. (2.5 to 5.5 m behind you)
  • 4The roof's back half and its ridge. Behind the play space the same striped tin rises on to its ridge over the house's back wall, 6.3 m above the deck. It is the far end of the roof you can stand on, and it is out of reach: anything behind the play plane is. (1.2 to 5.5 m behind you)
  • 5The station's end frame and line 1's bull wheel. A lattice frame on the station's east end, 2.5 m back, rising from the scaffold below. On it, 2 m back, is the greyed bull wheel that line 1's cable is driven by: 4.4 m across, no cream on it. Today that wheel stands 1.1 m back, inside the play space. It moves 5.0 m east and 2.9 m down to sit inside the loop, and back to 2 m. (2.0 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 6The station's wind engine. A cup wheel 5 m across on a mast on the end frame, 3 m back, turning in the wind off the glacier. A belt runs down from it to the bull wheel. It is why a car comes every six seconds whether anybody is waiting or not. (3.0 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 7The cliffs' haul cable. The thick black cable from the cliffs' bull wheel comes up under the loop to a pulley at the foot of the end frame, and on up the frame to the engine. The same wind turns both. (2.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 8Line 1's cable and its guide wheels. One loop of black greased cable, 0.15 m thick, 2 m behind you, with a greyed guide wheel 1 m across at each corner. The whole road of both cars is on screen, so a car that comes is seen coming. Today the loop is two thin wires 0.5 m back, inside the play space. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 9The cars' carriages. Each car's hanger cranks back at its head to a carriage on the cable, 2 m behind you. The head and the carriage are scenery, greyed. The hanger under them is held. (1.2 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 10Pylon 1. The first tower: two legs 3 m apart and zigzag braces, rising from below the frame. Today it is a card 1.2 m back, at the edge of the play space. It goes to 2.5 m. Behind its perch stands the frog's parked gondola, which has its own page. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 11Pennants and a streamer. A line of pennants from the middle tower to the end frame, 2 m back and more than 3 m above the highest hand, and a streamer on pylon 1's top. They all point east. The wind here does not push you: it turns the engine, and the pennants show it. (2.0 to 2.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 12The step and the rack on every car. Every car has a boarding step out of its west end, 0.9 m long, and a freight rack out of its east end, 2.4 m long. Both are solid today and neither is drawn (note 77). They are modelled at exactly those lengths, in rust-orange iron with a cream band along the top. The roof's edge and the floor's edge get the same band: cream is the edge you take.
  • 13Warning stripes on the deck's end. East of the gangway the deck runs on for 0.7 m at 688.0 m. A car's step comes down to 1.65 m above that, which is less than your height. So the deck's end wears the slanted yellow and black stripes (note 78): stand on the gangway, not down here.
  • 14The cars' hangers. Each car hangs from an upright pole on the middle of its roof: 1.5 m tall, 0.12 m thick, solid and held, as in the course today. It is drawn as a hold: the cableway's red with a cream band round its top 0.5 m, where a hand takes it. It is the way up to pylon 1's perch: at the top of the car's rise the hanger's top stops 1.06 m under the grating.
  • 15The station's roof. Real (note 77): striped red and cream tin on timber boards, 0.3 m thick, from x -251.0 to -237.6, its top 4.8 m above the deck. It can be held and stood on, and a cream band runs along its edges. You get onto it only from a car: as a car comes down past the roof's east end, its step passes 1.0 m out from the edge, and you step off west onto the tin. From up there the same step can be taken again on the next car, higher and earlier than from the gangway. It leads nowhere else: the nearest thing above it, a car on the high leg, is 4.9 m up. A fall from it lands on the deck.

Small effects, no models

  • The bull wheel, the four guide wheels and the wind engine turn at the cars' own pace, all the time.
  • Gold dust in the low sun, and the car's long shadow sliding over the station's roof as it comes down.
  • A creak from the step as it takes your weight. A dull boom from the tin under your boots on the roof.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 77: every car's step (0.9 m) and rack (2.4 m) are drawn. Neither moves: they are where the course has them.
  • Note 77, settled: the roof is made real. A new solid roof of tin on timber, 0.3 m thick, from x -251.0 to -237.6 (13.4 m), its top at 692.8 m: 4.8 m above the deck and 4.1 m above the gangway. It can be held and stood on. It takes the place of the peaked roof card (18.5 m wide, 1 m back, 695.4 to 701.0 m) and of the roof on the station model, which both go.
  • How the roof is reached, and why it needs no guard: only from a car's step as the car comes down past the roof's east end, 1.0 m out from it. From the deck and the gangway it is out of reach, by 0.5 m even from the top of the rail post. From the roof you can take the next car's step higher and earlier, ride it down, and go out east the way challenge 8.1 already sends you. Nothing else is within 4.9 m of it. So it skips nothing, and 8.1 is as hard as it was. It is not tarred.
  • Note 77: the station house stands 2.5 m back, 12 m wide, under the roof's back half. Today it is 1.2 m back and 3 m deep, so its front half is in the play space. The pug sign moves from 1.0 m back onto the house's wall, the ticket booth from 1.4 m back to against it, and the lit lantern beside the sign goes cold.
  • Note 80, settled: the two cars' hangers, 1.5 m tall and 0.12 m thick, stay solid and held, as in the course today. They are drawn as holds, with a cream band round their top 0.5 m. Pylon 1's perch is reached as today, from the top of a car's hanger. The iron rung an earlier pass of this page hung under the perch is taken out. Nothing is added to hold.
  • Note 78, settled: a crunch knocks you out only when the head or the body is pinned. The roof's east end and a passing car are never closer than 1.0 m, so the roof makes no pinch.
  • Note 78: one pinch is kept and striped: the deck's last 0.7 m, east of the gangway, under a car's step.
  • Note 76: what is drawn is what is touched. The scenery bull wheel is greyed, has no cream and stands 2 m back, so it is not taken for the cliffs' wheel that you ride.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: line 1's two wires (0.5 m back) become one cable loop 2 m back. Of its two bull wheels (1.1 m back), one moves to the end frame as said above and the one on pylon 1 becomes two guide wheels. Pylon 1's tower card goes from 1.2 m back to 2.5 m. The 6 m pennant line inside the loop (0.6 m back) goes up to 704 m and back to 2 m.
  • Also in this frame, from the cliffs' file: a pennant line 26 m long that hangs 1 m back at head height, from the deck out past pylon 1. It goes back with the house, along its wall under the roof, 2.5 m back.
  • Note 89: the pennants and the streamer point east and the engine turns. No wavy wind lines here, because this wind does not push.
