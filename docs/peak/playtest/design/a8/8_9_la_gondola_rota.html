El Teleférico · Area and background design

Challenge 8.9: La Góndola Rota

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Pylon 3 is the junction. The line from the valley ends on its head, and the four express lines to the high station start 16 m to the west, at their own tower. Between the two ran a shuttle: one gondola on a rail under the long girder, to and fro. One of its two arms broke. The car now hangs from the other by a corner of its roof, crooked, west end low, with its back door hanging open, and it swings when anything moves on it. It is still the only way across. In by the east window, out by the roof hatch, down to the rack on its low end, swing it, and jump: 4 m west to the rack of the first express car, which waits there for a second and a half.

What must not change

  • The dead car is a real hanging body of 120 kg on one hinge. It hangs about 30 degrees off level and swings only when you move on it.
  • The way through it: the east window, 1 m high. The roof hatch, 0.9 m. The rack on its low west end, 1.6 m.
  • The jump: 4.1 m west and 0.6 m down, to the rack of express line 1's car, which waits 1.5 seconds at its east end every 20 seconds.
  • The grate under that car, 1.6 m of iron at 746.5 m, for anyone who would sooner land on something still.
  • Pylon 3's head and the rail post you lean out from.

Background

  • 1Pylon 3 and the express tower. Two lattice towers 2.5 m back. Pylon 3 is a card 1.2 m back today. The express tower is new: today the four express cables end in the air at x -214.5, with nothing holding them and nothing under the grate. It stands under the grate, with an arm, a buffer and a greyed guide wheel at the end of each line. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 2The long girder: the shuttle's rail. A lattice girder 2.5 m back at 754.6 m, 23 m long, from the express tower across pylon 3 to the counterweight frame. The shuttle ran on a rail under it, between a buffer at each end. A shaft inside it carries the wind engine's turn to a winding drum on the express tower. A 7.2 m length of it, over the dead car, is a solid iron beam in the play space today. That is 5.5 m above the head and out of reach, so nothing changes in the play when it goes back with the rest. (2.0 to 2.9 m behind you)
  • 3The arm that held, and the arm that broke. Both of the car's arms went up to carriages on the rail. The east one still holds: it is scenery now, greyed and behind you. The west carriage is still on the rail, with a sheared stub of arm 0.7 m long under it. (1.2 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 4The car's back door. On the car's back wall, 1.2 m behind you: a door with the peeling pug crest, hanging open by its top hinge. It is the back of the car, seen through the open side, and it is never in your way. (1.2 to 1.4 m behind you)
  • 5Express lines 1 to 3, and the car's arm. Three of the four express cables, 2 m behind you, going up west out of the frame from the tower's arms. The first express car hangs from the lowest by its arm, which is scenery. (1.2 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 6The one who tried. A frozen climber sitting at the back of the head, with his back to the tower, looking at the dead gondola. He is the only one on the cableway. (1.3 to 1.7 m behind you)
  • 7Pylon 3's wind engine and a streamer. Above the girder, 3 m back: the cup wheel that hauls the express lines, its belt coming down to the girder's shaft. At the back of the head, a streamer pole pointing east. (1.5 to 3.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The dead car, every solid part drawn. It is the same car as the ones that run, and the model shows every part you can touch at its true size: the roof in two pieces with the hatch between, the floor, the two low end walls, and the rack on its west end, 1.6 m long, which is solid today and not drawn (note 77). Cream bands on the roof's edges, the hatch's rim, the floor's edge and the rack.
  • 9No longer solid: the car's arm and the crossarm. The car's one arm, 1.5 m long and 0.12 m thick, and the crossarm it hangs from, an iron beam 7.2 m long at 754.6 m. Both are solid today. Both become scenery. Nothing in the route uses either.
  • 10Warning stripes on the head's west end. The car's east corner hangs 1.4 m from the rail post, and a hard swing brings it in over the head's edge. So the post and the last 0.6 m of the head wear the slanted yellow and black stripes (note 78).
  • 11The express car's rack and step. The rack you jump to, 2.4 m, and the short step on the car's far end, 0.6 m: both drawn at the length they are, with a cream band (note 77).

Small effects, no models

  • The car groans on its one arm when you land on it, and the open door swings a beat behind the car.
  • Flakes of paint and a little rust fall from the carriage each time the car swings.
  • The camera stands back as you reach the rack, and shows the drop under the jump.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 77: the dead car's rack, 1.6 m, is drawn. So are the express car's rack and step.
  • The sheet's call from note 80: the dead car's arm (1.5 m long, 0.12 m thick) and the crossarm above it (7.2 m long, 0.25 m thick, at 754.6 m) stop being solid and go behind. Nothing in the route uses either.

  • To check when the crunch rule is built: a climber standing on the grate has 2 cm of headroom under the express car's step when it stops. The route asks you to stand there and reach up, so it is not striped.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: pylon 3's card goes from 1.2 m back to 2.5 m. The express lines' wires (0.5 m back) become cables 2 m back and end on the new express tower. The lantern under the head goes cold.
