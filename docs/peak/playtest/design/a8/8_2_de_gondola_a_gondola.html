Challenge 8.2: De Góndola a Góndola

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Two loops of cable stand side by side over La Garganta, 12 m apart. The first comes from the station and turns back at pylon 1. The second goes on east. When the cableway worked you changed from one to the other on a landing that hung between the two towers: you stepped off a car of the first loop as it rose past, and onto a car of the second. The landing fell in the winter the cable snapped, and its two broken brackets still reach toward each other. What is left is what the landing was built round. Every six seconds a car of each loop starts up its tower at the same moment, and the two rise side by side for 2.9 seconds, 6.1 m apart, rack toward step. One shaft turns both loops, which is why they never fall out of step. The camera stands back here: it shows both loops and 22 m of the drop.

What must not change

  • Both loops and all four cars, with the course's own numbers: 12 m apart, the same lap, turning opposite ways.
  • The window: every six seconds a car of line 1 starts up its east end as a car of line 2 starts up its west end. They rise side by side for 2.9 seconds.
  • The gap: 6.1 m, level, from the tip of line 1's rack to the tip of line 2's step. A miss is La Garganta.
  • Pylon 1's perch, where the challenge starts.

  • 3The two loops of cable and their wheels. Each loop is one black greased cable, 0.15 m thick, 2 m behind you, with a greyed guide wheel 1 m across at each corner and one greyed bull wheel, 4.4 m across, inside its driven end. The whole road of all four cars is on screen. Today the cables are thin wires 0.5 m back and the wheels stand 1.1 m back, all inside the play space. (2.0 to 2.3 m behind you)
  • 4The link girder and its shaft. A lattice girder between the tops of pylon 1 and line 2's west tower: 15 m long at 708 m, 3.9 m above the highest hand. A shaft runs along it. A belt brings the turn up from line 1, and a crossed belt takes it down to line 2's bull wheel. The crossed belt is why line 2 turns the other way. The one shaft is why the two loops can never drift apart. (2.3 to 2.9 m behind you)
  • 5The cars' carriages. One carriage on the cable for each car, 2 m behind you, with the hanger's cranked head coming back to it. Scenery, greyed. The hanger under it is held. (1.2 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 6The fallen change landing. Two broken bracket arms, one on each tower at 694 m, each 1.7 m long and torn off short. They held the landing where passengers changed lines. They are 2.5 m back and greyed, and they mark the height and the width of the jump. Nothing hangs from them. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 7Pennants, a streamer and a wind sock. A line of pennants under the girder, 2 m back and 3.2 m above the highest hand. A streamer on pylon 1's top and a wind sock on line 2's west tower. All point east, the way you are about to jump. (2.0 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 8The snapped cable's end. On the east tower's arm, high out of reach: the frayed end of the cable that went on to pylon 2. It belongs to challenge 8.3, and so do the cable, the clamp plate and the stiff end drawn in rust at the right of this sketch: all three go. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)

Play space

  • 9Rack and step, drawn. The two things this jump is made of are solid today and not drawn (note 77): line 1's rack, 2.4 m, and line 2's step, 0.9 m. Both are modelled at those lengths with a cream band, and each ends in a cream knuckle, so the two tips show level across the gap the whole way up.
  • 10The four hangers, held. Each car hangs from an upright pole on the middle of its roof, 1.5 m tall and 0.12 m thick: solid and held, as today, in the cableway's red with a cream band round its top 0.5 m. Line 1's hanger is how you get to the perch where this challenge starts: its top stops 1.06 m under the grating. No rung is added.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 77: the rack and the step are drawn, on all four cars. Neither moves.
  • Note 80, settled: the four cars' hangers, 1.5 m tall and 0.12 m thick, stay solid and held, as today, and are drawn with a cream band round their top 0.5 m. The perch is reached from a line 1 car's hanger, as today. The iron rung an earlier pass put under the perch (0.8 m at 701.2 m) is taken out. Nothing in this area's course changes for it.
  • Note 83, settled: when you hold line 1's rack and line 2's step at once, you keep the one more of your limbs are on, and then the higher. A rule of play. Nothing is drawn for it.
  • Note 78: no pinch is kept here. One place to check when the crunch rule is built: inside each loop, the rack of the car going down and the step of the car going up pass 0.1 m apart. A crunch knocks you out only when the head or the body is pinned, and a hand caught there slips free.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: line 2's wires (0.5 m back) become one cable loop 2 m back. Line 2's west bull wheel (1.1 m back) moves 2.2 m east and 3.4 m down to sit inside the loop, 2 m back. Its east bull wheel becomes two guide wheels. The 6 m pennant line inside line 2's loop (0.6 m back) goes up under the girder. The three tower cards go from 1.2 m back to 2.5 m. Pylon 1's top goes from 706 m to 708 m to carry the girder, and line 2's east tower from 708 m to 712 m to carry the snapped cable's arm.
  • Note 89: pennants, streamer and wind sock point east. No wavy wind lines, because this wind does not push.
