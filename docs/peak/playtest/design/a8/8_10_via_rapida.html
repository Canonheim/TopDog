El Teleférico · Area and background design

Challenge 8.10: Vía Rápida

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The express lines: four cables, one over the next, 5.1 m apart, climbing 47.5 m west and 22.5 m up from the express tower to the high station. One car on each. They were the fast way to the glacier, and they run in pairs, so as one car goes up the next one comes down and they pass in the middle. The middle is marked by the tall tower that carries all four cables. Every ten seconds, in front of that tower, each car passes the car of the line above it, at 12 m a second between them. Standing on a car's roof with a hand up, the short step on the end of the car above takes you. Three times, and the fourth car carries you to the high station. The camera stands far back here: it shows 28 m of height, so both cars' whole roads are in sight. This sketch is 52 m wide for the same reason.

What must not change

  • Four lines, 5.1 m one over the next, each 47.5 m across and 22.5 m up. One car on each at 6 m a second, waiting 1.5 seconds at each end. Lines 1 and 3 start at the east end, lines 2 and 4 at the west.
  • The transfer: the step on the upper car, 0.6 m long, clears the lower car's cable by 0.43 m. Standing on the lower roof your head is just under it and your raised hand is 0.2 m over it. The course's second way stays: up the lower car's hanger and onto the upper car's floor behind the step, 0.25 m higher.
  • Three transfers, in the middle of the span, then the ride on line 4 to its west end.
  • The grate at 746.5 m, where the challenge starts.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The mountain's shoulder. On the west of the frame, 12 m back: the flat cut-out of the mountain that began on the cliffs, carried on up. Here it is dark scree with snow lying in its gullies. It is the only thing of ours behind the lines. East of it there is the far view and nothing else. (12.0 to 14.0 m behind you)
  • 2The four cables. Black greased cable, 0.15 m thick, 2 m behind you, from the express tower's arms up to the high station. Today they are thin wires 0.5 m back, inside the play space, and they stop in the air at both ends. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 3The middle tower. It stands on the low station far below and comes up through this frame, exactly behind the place where the cars pass. It has four arms, one for each cable, each with a saddle wheel, and a pug crest. A car's arm goes by in front of an arm of the tower at the moment of the transfer, so you know where to be ready before a car is anywhere near. Today it is a card 1.6 m back. It goes to 2.5 m. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 4The express tower. At the lines' east end, 2.5 m back: a lattice tower with the grate on its side, an arm, a buffer and a greyed guide wheel for each line, and the winding drum on the end of the long girder that the wind engine on pylon 3 turns. It is new. Today nothing holds the cables' east ends. (2.0 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 5The cars' carriages. One carriage on the cable for each car, 2 m behind you, with the hanger's cranked head coming back to it. Scenery, greyed. The clack you hear as a car crosses the middle tower is the carriage on its saddle wheel. (1.2 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 6One line of pennants, above the top cable. Today two lines of pennants, each 40 m long, hang 0.6 m back, inside the play space. The lower one runs between lines 2 and 3, exactly where a hand goes up for a step. It goes. The upper one already runs 4.5 m above the top cable, where no hand comes. It stays there and goes back to 2 m. It streams east, down the lines. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7Step and rack on all four cars. Each express car has a short step out of its west end, 0.6 m, and a rack out of its east end, 2.4 m. Both are solid today and neither is drawn (note 77). The step is the whole transfer, so it is modelled at exactly 0.6 m with a cream band on its top and a cream lip underneath, where a raised hand meets it.
  • 8The roof you stand on. A car's flat roof, 2.6 m long, with a cream band along both long edges. Stand tall on it with a hand up.
  • 9The hangers. An upright pole on the middle of each car's roof, 1.5 m tall and 0.12 m thick, held, as in the course today. It is the cableway's red with a cream band round its top 0.5 m. It is something to steady yourself on while you stand tall on the roof, and the second way to transfer: up it, and onto the floor of the car above as it passes, 0.25 m higher than the step.
  • 10The grate. Open iron grating, 1.6 m, on the express tower's side, under the place where the first car's step stops.

Small effects, no models

  • The first snowflakes begin above 770 m and thicken toward the high station. The course has them today.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 77: the step (0.6 m) and the rack (2.4 m) are drawn on all four express cars. Neither moves.
  • Note 80, settled: the four cars' hangers, 1.5 m tall and 0.12 m thick, stay solid and held, as today, and are drawn with a cream band round their top 0.5 m. So the course's second way to transfer stays: up the lower car's hanger to the upper car's floor. Nothing in this area's course changes for it.
  • Note 83, settled: when you hold your own car and the step of the car above at once, you keep the one more of your limbs are on, and then the higher. The higher is the car you want.
  • Note 78: no pinch is kept. A car passing over a roof clears a standing head by 0.06 m, which is the challenge itself, and a hit at that speed already knocks you out.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: the four wires (0.5 m back) become cables 2 m back. Of the two 40 m pennant lines (0.6 m back), the lower one, between lines 2 and 3, goes. The upper one stays 4.5 m above the top cable and goes back to 2 m. The middle tower's card goes from 1.6 m back to 2.5 m. The express tower is added, 2.5 m back.
  • Note 89: the pennants stream east. No wavy wind lines, because this wind does not push.
