El Teleférico · Area and background design

Challenge 8.5: Sillas Voladoras

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The chair line: four plank benches on one cable, from the shed on pylon 2's head down to the foot ledge on El Colmillo, 16 m away and 12 m lower. It was the linesmen's way out to the fang, and it was wound by hand from the shed. Nobody winds it now, so the chairs hang where they stopped, and the wind off the glacier swings them, each a little out of step with the next. You cross on the seats. Their poles are behind you: each is fixed to the back of its seat and goes up to the cable 2 m back. Under the chairs is the whole of La Garganta.

What must not change

  • Four chairs, each on a pole of 3.2 m, swinging 20 degrees each way every 4 seconds, out of step with each other as the course has them.
  • The seats: 1.2 m wide and 0.25 m thick. After note 80 they are the only things to hold.
  • The three reaches: from the head's east end to the first seat, seat to seat, and from the fourth seat to the fang's rib.
  • The foot ledge on the fang, 1.3 m wide, is the only stance on the far side.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The chair line's cable. One black greased cable, 0.15 m thick, 2 m behind you, from a greyed guide wheel under the shed's eave down to another on the fang. From the eave it runs back into the shed to the hand winch. It does not move. Today it is a thin wire 0.5 m back. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 2The four poles. Each pole is 3.2 m long and 0.12 m thick. Today it is solid: it can be held, and a pole swinging past can bat you. It becomes scenery (note 80). It is fixed to the back edge of its seat, cranks back out of the play space, and rises to the cable 2 m behind you, in the greyed red. You pass in front of it. (1.3 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 3The chair shed on pylon 2. Where the chairs were boarded: a board wall 2.5 m back under the tarred roof, with a bench, the hand winch that wound the line, a cold lantern and the pug crest. The tower's lattice is behind it. (1.9 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 4The cable's anchor on the fang. An iron arm bolted into the fang's body, 2 m back, with the guide wheel and a pug plate. The cable is made fast here, 5 m above the foot ledge. (1.9 to 2.3 m behind you)
  • 5Pennants on the chair cable. The 12 m pennant line stays on the chair cable and goes back with it, from 0.6 m behind you to 2 m. The pennants hang 0.7 m, which leaves them clear of a hand held up from a seat. They blow east, toward the fang. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 6The zip line's buffer, above. At the back of the maintenance deck on the roof's ridge: the post the zip line comes home to. It belongs to challenge 8.7. (2.0 to 2.2 m behind you)

Play space

  • 7The four seats. A plank bench with a cream front edge, no back and no arms, modelled at exactly 1.2 m by 0.25 m. The seat is all of the chair that you can touch.
  • 8The rib and the foot ledge. White chalk, dry and matte, on the fang's dark rock: a rib 3.4 m tall that the fourth chair swings to, and a ledge 1.3 m wide under it. They are the only white things on this side of the fang below its chalk holds.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • The chairs are the wind's tell here: they swing because it blows, and their pennants stream east.
  • Each seat creaks at the end of its swing.
  • Gold dust drifting through the chairs, and the town in the valley straight under the second one.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 80: the four poles, each 3.2 m long and 0.12 m thick, stop being solid and go behind the climb. The seats do not change: the three reaches of this challenge are measured between seats and stand as they are. A seat is now the only thing to catch on a miss.
  • Note 83: seat to seat, you keep the seat more of your limbs are on, or the higher one. A rule of play. Nothing is drawn for it.
  • Note 78: no pinch is kept here. One place to check when the crunch rule is built: the seats of chairs 1 and 2, and of 3 and 4, close to 0.5 m of each other at the ends of their swings, where they are almost still.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: the chair line's wire (0.5 m back) becomes a cable 2 m back, and its 12 m pennant line (0.6 m back) goes with it.
  • Note 89: the swinging chairs and their pennants show the wind. No wavy lines, because this wind does not push you.
