Challenge 8.3: El Cable

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

This was the long span: one cable from line 2's east tower up to pylon 2, 13 m of it, that carried the cars across. It snapped in the winter the ice came. A short frayed end hangs from each tower, high out of reach, and between them there is only air. So where the cable was, you jump: off the freight rack of line 2's car as it reaches the top of its loop, 10.7 m across and 4.7 m up, to the west end of pylon 2's landing. The car is moving east at that moment, which helps. Under the jump is La Garganta, and under that the town.

What must not change

  • It is one jump, with nothing to hold between the car and the landing (note 79).
  • Where it starts: the tip of the rack on line 2's car, at the top of the loop's east end, 698.3 m up.
  • Where it ends: the west end of pylon 2's landing, 5.4 m of iron grating at 703.0 m. The landing does not move.
  • Pylon 2's two legs under the landing stay slick, so nobody who falls short climbs the pylon from below.

Small effects, no models

  • The two frayed ends stir in the wind, slowly. They are thick and stiff, and they do not swing like a rope.
  • Gold dust drifting east across the gap, the way you jump.
  • The camera stands back for the jump and shows the whole drop under it.
