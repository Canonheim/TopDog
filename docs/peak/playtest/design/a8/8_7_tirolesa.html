Challenge 8.7: Tirolesa

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The zip line was the linesmen's way home from the fang: one cable from the post on El Colmillo's top down to the maintenance deck on pylon 2's roof, with a trolley on it and a handle bar under the trolley. You reach up from the plate, take the bar, and it goes: 27 m west over La Garganta in three seconds, with the town straight under your boots. The line used to end in the air, 4 m short of the deck. It is whole again. It runs all the way, and the trolley comes to rest against a sprung buffer over the middle of the deck, where you let go and stand. The camera stands well back for this: the fang, the whole line and the deck are in one frame.

What must not change

  • The trolley waits 2.7 m over the plate with its bar 1.8 m over it. It goes when you take the bar, runs at 9 m a second, and goes home 6 seconds after you leave it.
  • The plate and the post on the fang's top, and the maintenance deck: 6 m of planks at 720.0 m.
  • Nothing is under the ride.

  • 2The fang's far horn and the top's fittings. The fang's body, 1.5 to 4 m back, rising 2 m behind the plate. On it: the post's back stay, the linesman's box, a cold lantern, and a streamer pointing east. The cream bar hangs in front of dark rock, so it shows. (1.3 to 4.0 m behind you)
  • 3The zip line. A black greased cable 0.15 m thick, 2 m behind you, from the back of the post on the fang to the buffer over the deck: 26.8 m across and 5.1 m down. The trolley's wheels run on it and are scenery. A stem cranks forward from them to the bar you hold. (2.0 to 2.1 m behind you)

Play space

  • 9The handle bar. A cream bar 0.7 m wide on a stem 0.9 m long. The bar and the stem are held. Nothing else of the trolley is: its body, 0.5 m by 0.22 m and solid today, goes back to the line and is scenery.
  • 10The bar's new road. Today the trolley stops dead at (-186.0, 725.1), 4 m short of the deck. Its road now runs on 7.0 m further west, to (-193.0, 723.0), over the middle of the deck (note 82). The whole line is hung a little steeper to end there: where it used to stop, it is 0.8 m lower than it was. The thin broken end that hangs 4 m off the old stop goes.
  • 11Where you step off. The planks under the buffer are worn pale by years of boots, 2 m of them. The place to let go is drawn on the deck.

Small effects, no models

  • The trolley's wheels sing on the cable, rising as it speeds up and falling as it slows over the deck.
  • The buffer's spring gives 0.3 m and comes back, with a clank.
  • The town, tiny, passing straight under your boots in the painted valley.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 82: the zip line goes all the way. The trolley's road is 7.0 m longer and ends at (-193.0, 723.0), against a sprung buffer over the middle of the deck. There is no timed let-go: you ride, it slows, you stand.
  • Note 82, settled: the challenge is a plain catch and ride, and stepping off. Parker took this design's answer, so nothing is added on the way in. Its difficulty is 4 today and comes down. It is to be rated again.
  • To measure when it is built: that your boots clear the deck's east lip. With the bar at 722.7 m there, a hanging body has about 0.5 m to spare.
  • Note 77, the rule: only the bar and the stem are solid, and both are drawn. The trolley's body stops being solid.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: the zip wire (0.5 m back) becomes a cable 2 m back. The 16 m pennant line along it (0.6 m back) goes, because a trolley runs there. The lit lantern over the deck (0.8 m back) goes cold and the toolbox (0.7 m back) goes to 1.3 m.
