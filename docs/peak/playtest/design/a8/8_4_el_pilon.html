El Teleférico · Area and background design

Challenge 8.4: El Pilón

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

Pylon 2 is the cableway's big tower, the one everything else is hung from. The long span came in on its west side, the chair line leaves from its east, the zip line comes home to its roof, and the counterweights ride above it. You climb it by its fittings. From the landing you take the lower wheel, which is turning, and let it throw you up and east to the upper wheel, which turns the other way. That one throws you up onto the head: 4 m of planks under a tarred tin roof, where people once waited for a chair. A wind engine high on the tower turns both wheels through belts. With the long span gone the wheels carry no cable at all. They turn because nobody stopped them.

What must not change

  • The landing at 703.0 m, the head at 714.5 m, and the two wheels where they are: the lower one's hub at (-194.6, 705.8), the upper one's at (-190.9, 710.3).
  • Both wheels are 2 m across and turn 9 times a minute, opposite ways: the lower one goes up on its east side, the upper one on its west.
  • After note 81 there is nothing else to hold: 3.7 m of air from rim to rim, and 2.8 m from the top of the upper rim to the underside of the head.
  • The tarred roof over the head stays slick, so nobody climbs from the head to the maintenance deck through it.

The three layers

Background

  • 1Pylon 2's lattice. Two legs 3 m apart and zigzag braces, a flat cut-out in the greyed red. Today it is a card 1.2 m back, at the edge of the play space: it goes to 2.5 m. Where the brace and the rung were bolted on, two pug plates are left with empty bolt holes. (2.5 to 2.8 m behind you)
  • 2The shaft and the two belts. Each wheel's axle runs back to a bracket on the tower. On the tower's centre line at 713 m sits a short shaft with a pulley, and the wind engine's belt comes straight down the inside of the tower to it. From the shaft one belt goes down to the lower wheel and a crossed belt to the upper one, which is why they turn opposite ways. Every belt runs inside the tower's width, over the lattice, so none hangs in open air beside a hold. (2.3 to 2.5 m behind you)
  • 3The chair shed. Under the tarred roof, 2.5 m back: a board wall in the greyed red below bare grey boards, with no cream on it, and a bench against it, a cold lantern, the peeling pug crest on the gable, and the hand winch that wound the chair line. The crest and the lantern are 0.9 and 0.8 m back today, inside the play space, and the lantern is lit. Both go to the wall, and the lantern goes cold. (1.9 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 4The long span's arm and its snapped end. A crossarm out to the west at 715 m, 2.5 m back, with 3 m of frayed cable hanging from its tip and a wind sock on it. It is what is left of challenge 8.3's cable, and it is 4 m and more from any hand. (2.0 to 2.6 m behind you)
  • 5The chair line leaving. East of the shed: the chair line's cable going down toward the fang over a greyed guide wheel under the eave, and the first chair's pole. They belong to challenge 8.5. (1.3 to 2.1 m behind you)
  • 6An oil can and a grease pot. At the back of the landing, under the lower wheel's bracket. Somebody was mending it. The oil can is 0.7 m back today: it goes to 1.3 m. (1.2 to 1.6 m behind you)

Small effects, no models

  • The belts run and the shaft turns, all the time, and each wheel's cream spokes show which way it is going.
  • Flakes of red paint come off the lower wheel's rim when you take it.
  • A dry squeal from the upper wheel once in every turn.

Tonight's notes folded in

  • Note 81: the brace and the rung come out, as listed in the play space above. The climb is landing, lower wheel, upper wheel, head. Rim to rim is 3.7 m of air, and from the top of the upper rim to the underside of the head is 2.8 m. To measure when it is built: that 9 turns a minute gives enough throw for both. Its difficulty goes up from 3 and is to be rated again.
  • Note 78: a crunch knocks you out. Nothing solid now stands near enough to either wheel to crush you against: the cable's end, which made the pinch, went with note 79. So no warning stripes are needed on this tower.
  • Note 76: each wheel is modelled at exactly 2 m, rim and spokes. Nothing solid lies outside what is drawn.
  • Note 79: no cable runs onto the lower wheel any more. The wheels are turned by belts from the wind engine, so they still have a reason to turn.
  • Scenery that moves out of the play space: the tower card goes from 1.2 m back to 2.5 m. The pug crest (0.9 m back) and the lantern (0.8 m back, lit) go to the shed's wall, 2.5 m back, and the lantern goes cold. The oil can goes from 0.7 m back to 1.3 m.
