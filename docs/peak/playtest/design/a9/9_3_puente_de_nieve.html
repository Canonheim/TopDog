El Glaciar · Area and background design

Challenge 9.3: Puente de Nieve

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The third leg, along the top of the slot. This crack once had a lid of snow at 804 m, from wall to wall. The shelf's top, the door's sill and the ice cave's floor are all at that height, and between the shelf and the door three pieces of the lid still hang across the crack. Each one sags in the middle and has 18 m of air under it. Two stones that came down when the shelf broke are wedged between the crack's walls and keep the three pieces apart. You step from the shelf's broken end across 1.2 m of open air onto the first bridge, and each piece of a bridge falls in a second after you take it. At the far end is the door of the ice cave: the palest thing in the slot, with a lantern burning inside it and one of the ones who tried hanging in its light.

What must not change

  • Three bridges of three pieces each. A piece falls in 1 second after it is taken and comes back after 10.
  • The two wedged stones between them: the only places to stop.
  • The door, its sill at 804 m, and the cave's floor beyond.
  • The fall: 18 m to the snow floor, at the foot of Hielo Azul.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The crack's far wall. The same painted wall, running on past the door into the cave. Along it at 804 m, behind the bridges, is a thin white frost line where the old lid met the wall. It is a mark and not a ledge: flush, and no wider than a hand. (2.4 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 2The door, lit. A doorway 4.5 m tall and 2.5 m deep through the slot's east wall, with white frost round its edge. The far wall round it is painted two steps paler than anywhere else in the slot, and the paler block reaches 7 m back along the bridges. So it is on screen from the shelf's top, before the door is. (2.4 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 3The lantern inside the door. A candle lantern on a hook, on the cave's side of the door, beside the first of the ones who tried. From the bridges you see its amber through the doorway, on him and on the snow of the sill. (1.4 to 1.6 m behind you)
  • 4The first of the ones who tried. Seen through the door: a slab of dull ice with a frozen climber at its front, his boots at hand height and one mitten raised. He belongs to La Cueva de Hielo. He is drawn here because he is what you are walking toward. (1.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 5The light down the gap. The paler band above the shelf's broken end. From up here it is the gap you just climbed through, and the first step of this leg is across it. (2.4 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 6The arrow sign under the gap. On the far wall under the shelf's broken end: the arrow sign that pointed you up through the gap. It belongs to Carámbanos. No sign is needed up here: the paler wall and the lantern's light are to the east, and the boot prints on the shelf's top go east. (1.4 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 7One who fell. Deep in the far wall under the middle bridge, head down: one of the ones who tried, a flat blue shape. He says what the bridges do before you step on one. (2.4 to 2.6 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8The bridges, redone. Nine pieces of snow lid, each 1.1 m long and 0.45 m thick, in three bridges, with the two wedged stones between them. The yellow shapes are where they go. The black strip blanks out where they are today. Each bridge sags 0.15 m in the middle, is thinner than firm snow, and has open air under it. The stones are grey-brown and dry, each wedged from the far wall to the near one.
  • 9The breakable look. Every piece is cracked across and has a corner gone: the mountain's one look for a thing that will break. Snow sifts from each one all the time, so the three bridges hang over three thin curtains of falling snow.
  • 10The step across. The 1.2 m of open air between the shelf's broken end and the first piece. It is the gap you came up through. Stepping over it is the first move of this leg.
  • 11The sill. Firm snow, flat on top with a square front edge and ice under it: the first thing since the shelf that will not fall. Boot prints cross it into the cave.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • Snow sifting from every bridge piece, and more of it from the one you are on.
  • A piece that falls drops whole, turns over, and bursts on the floor 18 m down. A new one grows across from the far wall in plain view.
  • The lantern's amber on the sill, seen from the first bridge.
