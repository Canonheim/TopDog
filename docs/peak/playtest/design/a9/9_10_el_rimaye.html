El Glaciar · Area and background design

Challenge 9.10: El Rimaye

Design only. Nothing here is built, and nothing is modelled until the whole mountain has been playtested.

The place, in one paragraph

The top of the glacier. Between the ice and the ridge's rock there is a gap, 6.3 m wide at the top, where the ice has pulled away from the mountain as it creeps downhill. Its floor is the chute, and the hole the snow slabs come out of is in its far wall. From the niche you go up the last 8 m of ice, on a tusk and two nubs, to the tower's tip. One of the ones who tried is there. He is frozen leaning out over the gap, his boots in the ice of the tip, one mitten stretched toward the ledge he never reached. His mitten is the last hold on the glacier, and the launch: one fling, 7 m across and 3.4 m up, onto the storm ridge. What you fly at is a cabin with a lamp in it.

What must not change

  • The tusk, the two nubs, and the frozen climber with his three grips, each exactly where it is.
  • The fling from his mitten: 7 m west and 3.4 m up, to the ledge at 886 m. The first metre of the ledge is glazed.
  • Nothing within 2.3 m of his mitten but his own body and the tip under his boots: the swing is open below, west and above.
  • The fall: the gap, the chute, the mouth, the slide, the lip.

The three layers

Background

  • 1The cloud. What is given: grey cloud, close, filling the frame above the glacier's top. The storm cloud card that stood in front of this place is gone (note 92). All cloud here is behind you. (7.8 to 8.0 m behind you)
  • 2The ridge's rock, across the gap. The mountain, 2.4 m back, behind the whole gap: wet slate in leaning slabs. It is the storm ridge's piece, and it runs on up behind the ledge. (2.4 to 4.0 m behind you)
  • 3The slab hole. In the ice wall under the ledge you are flinging to: the dark mouth the snow slabs come out of (note 93). One shows in it every five and a half seconds, 6 m under your flight. (1.2 to 3.4 m behind you)
  • 4The horn at his back. The last frozen climber has ice at his back like the others: a horn of the cornice, dull mid blue, leaning out over the gap at the angle he leans. It stops at the back edge of the play space. Nothing is in front of him. Today it is a pale card 0.9 m back. (1.2 to 2.4 m behind you)
  • 5The last cane, and his lantern. On the tower's top behind him: the last cane of the route, its paper flag streaming, and a lit candle lantern hung on it at head height. The lantern lights him and his mitten. From the niche below, that light is where you are going. (1.4 to 1.8 m behind you)
  • 6The second niche. Banded ice at the back of the niche under the tip, and the cane you crossed the cornice to. (1.5 to 3.0 m behind you)
  • 7On the ledge: the storm ridge's prayer pole and cabin. The ridge's rock under its prow, and on the ledge the first prayer pole, 4 m tall, its paper streaming. The pole is there today. The storm ridge's sheet puts the keeper's cabin beside it, 4.3 m wide, with one lamp and a frog (note 94). Both are that ridge's. The cabin is outlined here only so the two pages agree: its lamp is what you fling toward. (1.2 to 3.4 m behind you)

Play space

  • 8His mitten. Bone-white wool, a lump 0.35 m across, at the end of an arm at full stretch. It points at the ledge. His body and mask are frost grey. His boots and belt are warm like the mitten. He is the same piece as the six in the cave.
  • 9The fling. The line of the fling, from the course's own numbers. It is drawn here only to show the place. Under it is the gap, lit grey to its floor, so you see what a miss costs before you let go.
  • 10The tusk and two nubs. A blunt tusk of blue ice at the niche's mouth, 2.4 m long, and two nubs on the face above it. The last blue ice on the mountain.
  • 11The glazed nose of the ledge. The first metre of the ledge is a lump of clear ice over rock: dark and glossy with a white glint, the mountain's look for a thing you cannot hold. The firm snow starts behind it. You land past it.

Foreground (sparse)

Nothing.

Small effects, no models

  • His lantern flickers. Its amber is on him and nowhere else.
  • The last cane's flag and the prayer pole's paper stream the same way.
  • A slab leaving the hole under you as you hang from his mitten.
  • On the far side: the cabin's lamp and its smoke, the storm ridge's.
