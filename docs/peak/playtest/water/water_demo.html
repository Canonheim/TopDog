This shows the technique, not the game's art. Two cheap tricks together: the pool's top is a row of springs, so it makes real waves; and everything that pours or splashes is loose droplets that melt into one inked cartoon shape wherever they touch.

Try: click anywhere to drop a rock from that spot. Hold and drag through a pool to stir it. Watch the water pour over the lip, land on the slanted slab, run along it and pour off its end; the barrel rides the waves.
