The Peak of DeGayo: the things, rated

Build 0.3.104, luchador-ui, 2026-11-14. Every distinct model placed in the course (each stem once, wherever it is used): decos, the skins on solids, movers', ropes', zones' and boards' models, from stages/peak/course/a0..a11.json and st.json: 1221 stems. Scored 1 to 10 as a THING: shape, detail, character, finish, whether it reads as the real object from the game's side-on camera, and whether it matches the station's 9.5 pieces and the frogs. Pictures are the kit's bench front views (Top Dog Kit/previews/check), cropped; judged with the game frames in frames/ beside them. Layer: decos by their depth (behind z -1.25 background, past +1.25 foreground); skins and movers are play space. 'Added' is the date the model first landed in kit/models.

The scale. Parker's own calibration: the old gondola (cream window frame, pug medallion, rust-chipped red, grooved two-wheel carriage) is a 9; its re-make gondola_tele (a plain boxy red cabin, two dark windows, a thin hanger) is a 2. The station's buildings and props are 9.5. Roughly: 1-3 a plain slab, box, bar or striped card with no detail; 4-5 decent but plain; 6-7 good, with character; 8+ excellent, house style.

All things: mean 4.1. Things that landed 11-14 (the overnight run, 1081 of them): mean 3.8; everything older (140): mean 6.5.
