# The reviewer's log: the Peak quality run

The gate of `docs/peak/QUALITY_TO_9_PLAN.md` parts 3 and 4. Every model, painted backdrop and painted
set is scored from its maker's sheet; it lands only at **8.5 or more**. After it lands, a game frame of
its place is checked again (under 8.5 in the game: back to the maker). Scenes are scored as frames
against **9**, as in `LOOKS_RATING.html`.

## The scale (fixed; the same for every round)

- **9.5** the station's plaza houses and props. **9** the old `gondola`, the old `summit_bell`, the old
  `kite`, the First Dog mask. **8.5 to 9** the frogs. **2** `gondola_tele` (Parker's own call).
- 1 to 3: a slab, box, bar, line or flat card with no detail. 4 to 5: the right object, plain.
  6 to 7: good, with character, missing a level of detail or finish. 8: house standard with a flaw.
  **8.5: lands.** 9+: a standard piece.

## What every sheet is checked for

1. **Silhouette** reads as the real object at 13.7 m from the game camera (true size, not only enlarged).
2. **Three levels of detail:** the form, the secondary parts, the trim and wear.
3. **House character:** pug faces, lucha masks, papel picado, honest wear; matte painted colour with ink;
   sits beside the plaza houses and the frogs.
4. **The game's angled camera,** high and low: nothing that only lines up straight on.
5. **One connected thing** across the depths: what crosses background, play space and foreground joins
   (stalk to cap, trunk to branch, pipe to joint, string to tread).
6. **Drawing = solid** where it is held: the outline matches what the hand finds.
7. **Craft:** no z-fighting, no floating parts, no blank or flat cards, no stand-in boxes, no striping.
