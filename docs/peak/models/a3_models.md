# La Gruta de Cristal: the models to make

The model list for biome 3, from its dress file `tools/peak/areas/a3_dress.py` (245 decos, 10
bright crystals lit). Every stem here is NEW: not in `kit/models/` nor in `Top Dog Kit\models\`,
nor on the a0, a1 or a2 lists. Make each at exactly its size (metres, w x h x d; +z faces the
camera). The game rescales a landed model that is more than a quarter off. Reused from the kit
(not listed): `mirror_facet` (4, scaled), `crystal_cluster_a` (2), `_b` (1), `_c` (4),
`crystal_spire` (1), `frog_sit`, `rope_coil`, `spore_lantern`, `glow_moss`.

Makers: **A** buildings, walls and rock masses, far cards, ground slabs (here: the cave's rock,
and the big crystals). **B** props, plants, signs, small dressing (here: the crystals' small
growths, the mirror faces, the frog's things, the garden's first pieces). **C** timber, iron,
machines: **nothing in this biome** (Parker, 11-13: the cave is purely natural, no made thing).

The cave's look (the sheet `docs/peak/playtest/design/data/a3_biome.py`): crystal is REAL
SHAPE and rock is PAINT. Crystal grows in points: six-sided in section, ending in a point,
leaning the way it grew; in clusters, one big point with smaller ones round its root on a collar
of rough dark rock. Two kinds: **rough crystal** is pale lilac, frosted and matte, and looks like
rock: you can hold it; **clear crystal** is deep indigo glass, mirror-smooth with one hard white
glint: you can't. "It will break": pale crystal crazed with white cracks, a corner gone. "It
moves": crystal on crystal, polished bright where it rubs. **Scenery crystal** (further back) is
bigger, bluer and dimmer, its points turned away from the climb, never pale lilac. Light is
violet and ice-white from inside the crystals (COLOUR, never a glowing material; a3.py adds the
lights); the background deep indigo; the things you hold the palest things on screen. Rock is
painted (BRIEF.md "Walls and masses"): a few large slanted planes of rough dark rock, its grain
long wavy lines and bands of flat colour in three or four dark indigo tones, none as pale as a
hold, nothing painted narrowing to a point, no dark slot or recess cut into a face; the few real
breaks are the socket a big crystal grows from, a hollow where one has fallen out, an overhang
with its shadow painted under it; the outline uneven and never ruled. No square panel repeated.
No chain, no bronze, no carved stone, no timber, no iron anywhere.

"Origin: foot" means the middle of its base (x and z) at its lowest point; for long pieces, the
middle of its x span, the bottom of its box, the middle of its depth. "Top" means the middle of
its top (it hangs down from there). "Skin" means the model is the look of course solids (`skin=`
in a3.py, its FOR THE AREA FILE item 5): it fills them, z -1.2 .. 1.2, its outline is theirs, and
its size is theirs (never rescaled). "Back" means a crystal's body behind the play space: it
runs from z -1.25 back to the z given, and its front matches its skin's section there. `rot` in
the dress file is counter-clockwise as the camera sees it: make every piece upright and level
unless the row says it is made in place.
