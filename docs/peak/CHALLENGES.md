# The 111 challenges of the Peak of DeGayo

Read `PEAK_DESIGN.md` first. Each challenge has an id (area.number), a name, what it is,
the intended moves, a difficulty (D1 learn .. D5 brutal) and what a miss costs
(`S` slip, `B` back two or three, `A` the area's start, `P` the Pozo, `X` everything).
Fling tiers F1..F5 are in PEAK_DESIGN §2. An area's author may adjust sizes to make a
challenge work, but not its idea, its order, or its cost.

## A1 La Mina Vieja (-30 to 90 m): timber, rails, mud

Teaches: slick, small flings, crumbling, the first rope, movers. The room is the
waterfall cavern; the route spirals up round the fall. Most misses end in the Sump.

| 1.3 | Los Tablones | A plank catwalk round the cavern wall. Four of its planks creak and snap 1.2 s after you take them. | D2 | A |

| 1.7 | El Carrito Loco | A second cart on a ledge track that ends in a broken trestle. Ride it, and leap for the hanging chains before it goes over. | D3 | A |
